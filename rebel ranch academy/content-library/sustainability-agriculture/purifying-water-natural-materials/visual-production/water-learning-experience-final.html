5. Dirty Is Visible. Danger Isn't.

A successful-looking build is not proof. This investigation separates observation from verification.

Safe demonstration

Use only simulated dirty water made from clean water, potting soil, and small plant pieces. Do not drink the output. Compare settling, cloth, washed gravel, washed sand, and optional commercially sold activated carbon.

6. Compare methods across time and resources

People solved water problems with the materials, knowledge, infrastructure and risks they had. The useful question is not “old or modern?” It is “what function was this method trying to perform, what did it do well, and what did it leave unresolved?”

What about Moringa, copper, frequency, or the moon?

Moringa: seed proteins can help coagulate suspended material under some conditions; that is a mechanism to understand, not a blanket potable-water claim.

Copper: storage in copper vessels has antimicrobial evidence under specific conditions, but it is not a universal treatment for every contaminant or water source.

Acoustic/frequency claims: water can respond physically to vibration and frequency. That does not by itself establish a human-health treatment claim. Observation and health-effect evidence are different claims.

Lunar effects: the moon clearly affects ocean tides. Claims that lunar cycles produce a specific meaningful health effect through human body water require separate evidence and should not be inferred from tides alone.
