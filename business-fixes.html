Most Common Questions

Clear answers before you choose

Is this consulting?

Yes. The fixed-price Business Fixes provide operational consulting through a defined service with a specific problem, scope, price, timeline, deliverable, and completion point.

Ongoing Business Guidance is a monthly support option for owners who need continued planning, decision support, and operational judgment across changing issues.

Is the monthly guidance service another Business Fix?

No. Business Fixes address one defined problem through a one-time project. Ongoing Business Guidance provides access to strategic and operational support throughout the month.

Will I need new software?

Not automatically. We use the simplest workable tool and do not recommend new software until the process and actual requirement are clear.

Can the scope be expanded?

Yes, but work outside the confirmed Business Fix is defined and priced separately before it begins.

Monthly guidance may identify or plan a larger implementation project, but the implementation itself is not automatically included in the monthly access.

What happens after a Business Fix is delivered?

You receive the agreed solution, instructions, walkthrough, and next-step recommendation.

When the business needs continued decision support rather than another defined project, Ongoing Business Guidance may be the better next step.

What if I am not sure which service I need?

Contact us with the problem you can see. We will help determine whether it fits a defined Business Fix or requires a different kind of support before work begins.
