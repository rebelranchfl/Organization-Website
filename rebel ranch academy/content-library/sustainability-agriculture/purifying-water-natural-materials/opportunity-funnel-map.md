# RRA-2026-0001 — Opportunity Expansion & Learning Funnel Map

## Core rule

This project should operate as a **learning web**, not a single one-way sales funnel.

Every product or free resource must solve a real next problem. Upsells are justified only when the learner has genuinely reached the next decision, skill or implementation need.

Commerce sequence:

`education → diagnosis → required function → options → buy only where justified`

---

## Entry Question 1 — “How do I filter/purify my water?”

### Immediate answer
Before recommending a device, determine source, intended use and what is actually in the water.

### Funnel path
`Water Profile → What Should I Test? → Know Your Water → Results Interpretation → Treatment Job Map → Build Your Water System`

### Follow-on projects
- water testing;
- treatment technologies;
- UV/disinfection;
- safe storage;
- maintenance/retesting.

## Entry Question 3 — “What is the best countertop water filter?”

### RRA reframing
The learner has started with form instead of function.

### Funnel path
`What is your source? → What are you trying to remove/change? → What evidence do you have? → Which treatment job is required? → Which countertop forms perform that job?`

### Differentiation
RRA avoids generic brand rankings and instead teaches how to compare certification/performance claims against the learner's actual need.

# Product ladder

Purpose:
- mission access;
- safety foundation;
- search/SEO entry;
- demonstrate RRA quality;
- stop premature purchases.

Components:
- Water Profile starter;
- What Should I Test? selector;
- screening vs lab explainer;

Natural CTA:

Recommended role: primary paid application product.

Components:

- functional decomposition;
- personalized treatment-job map;
- resource inventory;
- substitution logic;
- build/buy planner;
- scenario-specific diagrams;
- cost/maintenance comparison framework;
- verification plan;
- reusable reference guide;
- Think Like a Rebel transfer exercises.

This is the strongest paid-value layer because it saves decision time and turns information into implementation.

## Required recommendation test

Before a product/service recommendation enters public material, answer:
1. What exact function is being purchased?
2. Why can't the learner safely/reliably perform that function with what they have?
3. What performance/certification evidence matters?
4. Is the purchase optional, recommended, or required for the scenario?
5. What lower-cost alternative exists?
6. What ongoing consumable/maintenance cost follows?

# Cross-Academy transfer

The water project naturally feeds other learning areas because the underlying skill is problem decomposition.

### Personal Strength & Independence
- decision-making;
- resourcefulness;
- confidence through competence;
- critical thinking.

### Business & Operations
- define requirements before buying software/equipment;
- separate function from vendor/product;
- identify bottlenecks;
- build minimum viable systems;
- verify performance.

### Money & Finance
- distinguish need from marketed form;
- compare upfront and recurring cost;
- avoid unnecessary purchases;
- understand total cost of ownership.

### Family, Community & Leadership
- household contingency planning;
- shared maintenance responsibility;
- teaching children/teens how systems work.

## Funnel conclusion

The revenue opportunity is not created by forcing more products. It is created because one real-life problem exposes connected skills the learner genuinely needs next. The Academy should make those next steps visible and easy to enter.
