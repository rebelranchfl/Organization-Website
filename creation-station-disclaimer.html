Creation Station Disclosures

Earnings & Opportunity Disclaimer

Creation Station is designed to provide creative guidance, educational tools, project structure, and opportunities to learn. These resources can support growth, but individual results depend on many factors and are never guaranteed.

Last updated August 3, 2026

No guaranteed earnings or business results

Creation Station and Rebel Ranch Ministries do not guarantee sales, profits, income, earnings, cost recovery, customer interest, or business success. Examples involving products, prices, costs, presentation, or selling are educational illustrations—not promises of a particular result.

Educational guidance, not professional advice

Creation Station introduces age-appropriate creative, planning, business, and financial concepts. Its materials are educational and do not replace individualized legal, tax, accounting, financial, safety, or other professional advice.

Creation Station Studio

Creation Station Studio is an optional paid personal creator-page offering. A Studio page can help present a creator's story, work, products or services, ordering information, contact choices, and approved links. Having a Studio page does not guarantee visitors, orders, sales, or income.

Marketplace participation is separate

Creation Station membership and Creation Station Studio do not provide automatic access to the Rebel Ranch Marketplace. Marketplace participation is a separate, free application and approval process for eligible sellers.

Parents, guardians, and minors

Work created by anyone under 18 remains under parent or guardian control. A minor may not independently publish work, open a public seller presence, or approve public visibility. Parents and guardians are responsible for reviewing participation, project suitability, and any public-facing choices.

Demos and planned features

Public previews demonstrate the intended style and flow of the Creation Station experience. Some tools may be limited, illustrative, available only with approved access, or clearly marked as coming soon. Availability and final features may change as the program develops.
