# RRA-2026-0003 — Visual Production Brief

**Status:** Draft for Owner Product Review; not yet authorized for Visual Production  
**AI-Agent:** ChatGPT/GPT-5.6 Sol  
**Session:** RRA Product Design Agent

## Visual objective

Make reasoning visible. The product must not become a stack of text cards or a generic school worksheet.

## Primary visual language

Use familiar scenes first:
- text message / family statement;
- social post / repeated headlines;
- receipt or business metric;
- group of people with one dissenter;
- source/original document and copies;
- evidence items moving toward or away from a claim.

Technical psychology terms are optional depth.

## Required explanatory visuals

1. **Mixed-claim split visual** — one sentence separated into factual, inference, value, prediction/recommendation layers.
2. **Two-label map** — claim type vs evidence strength shown as separate dimensions.
3. **Source-root / echo map** — many repeated posts collapse back to one origin; independent sources remain separate.
4. **Bias Mirror** — two-sided self/other pressure view with equal visual weight.
5. **Social-pressure map** — evidence path kept separate from information/belonging/norm/value-weighting pressure.
6. **Unanimous Room visual** — alone vs unanimous group vs one dissenter comparison.
7. **Confidence ladder** — strongly corroborated through unsupported assertion, with value judgment clearly separate rather than ranked as “weak.”
8. **My Decision Record** — living integrated result, not a blank printable pretending to be personalized.
9. **Transfer web** — family, money, business, leadership, media/learning connections.

## Interaction rules

- Multiple claim layers can coexist.
- Multiple sources can coexist.
- Multiple social-pressure pathways can coexist.
- All depth controls are real clickable controls; non-clickable labels must not use pill/capsule styling.
- `Why?`, research, examples and sources must return to the exact learner position and preserve state.
- Changing a source relationship must visibly update the echo map.
- Changing claim layers must update My Decision Record.

## Tone

Prestigious, polished, credible and grounded; not childish detective branding, not political-war-room aesthetics, not distressed rebellion.

## Accessibility / readability

- plain text remains readable without relying on color;
- diagrams have text labels and accessible explanations;
- interactive controls have clear focus/keyboard behavior;
- mobile layout keeps claim/source relationships understandable;
- printable Decision Record remains useful in grayscale;
- no clipped text;
- no decorative full-pill shapes for non-interactive status/labels.

## Final Product QA expectations

Visual Production must test the actual learner-facing product, not only confirm that these requirements appear in a brief. The owner should be able to start at the beginning, complete a real scenario, open deeper evidence, return without lost state, and inspect the finished My Decision Record.
