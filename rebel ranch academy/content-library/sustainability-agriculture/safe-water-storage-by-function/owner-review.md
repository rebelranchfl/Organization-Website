# Owner Research Review — RRA-2026-0004

## Project
**Containers Are Containers — Safe Storage by Function**

## What the Research Agent established
- A water container is only appropriate when its history, material, condition, closure, dispensing, cleanability and environment match the intended use.
- Potable-water storage needs strong recontamination controls: clean/tightly closed vessel, narrow opening or sanitary dispensing, no prior toxic-chemical contents, cleaning/sanitizing and protected storage.
- Emergency storage adds capacity, rotation, access-during-outage and single-point-of-failure questions.
- Livestock and agricultural uses need different standards rather than copied household-potable rules.
- Storage is an operating/maintenance system, not a one-time purchase.
- Product Design has enough research to build a container audit, use-based branch map, capacity planner, storage anatomy visual and failure-mode exercises.

## Evidence set
10 agent-vetted sources. Owner source-by-source review is optional; overrides remain binding.

## Known boundaries
The research deliberately does not claim one universal container material, one universal stored-water shelf life, or one human drinking-water standard for animals/irrigation.
