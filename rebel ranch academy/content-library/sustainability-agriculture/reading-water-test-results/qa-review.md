# Research QA — RRA-2026-0008

Project: **Reading Water Test Results Without the Confusion**  
Revision: 1 — Initial Research foundation  
Status: **PASS — READY FOR RESEARCH REVIEW**

## Evidence QA
- The central interpretation sequence is supported by current direct EPA and Florida public-health/regulatory sources plus direct analytical-method guidance.
- The source audit contains 14 agent-vetted sources; owner source-by-source review remains optional.
- Public-system regulatory values are not presented as private-well legal compliance requirements.
- MCL, MCLG, action level/treatment technique, and secondary/aesthetic guidance are distinguished by function.
- `ND` / non-detect is not taught as absolute zero; method sensitivity/reporting-limit context remains visible.
- Unit interpretation is explicit, including the 1,000-fold mg/L-to-µg/L risk.
- Bacteria presence/absence interpretation is separated from ordinary chemical concentration interpretation.
- Worked examples are bounded examples, not an autonomous all-contaminant diagnosis engine.
- Exact thresholds are identified as time-sensitive and must be refreshed from current evidence before learner-facing publication.

## Responsible Rebellion QA
PASS. The project teaches what the evidence allows without making missing learner-specific context a blanket barrier. Testing is used to interpret, confirm and verify—not as permission to educate.
