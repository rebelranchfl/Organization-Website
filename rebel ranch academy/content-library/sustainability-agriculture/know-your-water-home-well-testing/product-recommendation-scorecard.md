## Composite recommendation

**Average score: 4.7 / 5**

## Recommendation

**PURSUE NOW — advance into PRODUCT_WORKING as an interactive free + paid diagnostic/planning system, not a standalone ebook.**

Working structure for architecture testing:

1. **Free mission layer:** `What Should I Test — and Why?` — builds a useful first testing-question map and next evidence route.
2. **Paid core candidate:** `My Well Test Plan + Result-to-Next-Action System` — persistent multi-condition testing plan, route comparison, sample organizer, result-context record, confirmation/retest logic, treatment-job/source-correction handoff and recurring verification calendar.
3. **Later/bundle:** result literacy, treatment verification, post-flood/repair workflows, local-risk branches and annual well care where supported.

These are Product Design working recommendations, not owner-approved offer names or public promises.

## Why Rebel Ranch Academy?

Free sources already teach well care. Commercial services already sell laboratory panels. RRA's justified role is to perform the missing **systems-thinking work around the test**:

> **What question does this household actually need answered, what evidence route can answer it without false certainty or waste, what does the result change, and how will the household verify the correction and remember the system next year?**

That aligns with Think Like a Rebel without treating any institution, vendor or consumer kit as doctrine.

## What changes if the learner implements this?

The learner gains the ability to:
- distinguish routine, event-triggered and local-risk testing;
- know what to ask a lab/local health department and why;
- compare testing routes by evidence function, consequence, logistics and verified cost;
- avoid assuming broad/expensive means better;
- preserve sample/result context;
- understand when a result needs confirmation;
- define a treatment job rather than shop for a device from a guess;
- verify treatment/correction with evidence;
- maintain a living household testing history.

## Paid-value test

**What work does RRA do that the learner otherwise has to research, compare, design, calculate/test, organize or decide?**

RRA would:
- structure the source/use/risk/event/vulnerability diagnostic intake;
- convert it to a testing-question map;
- separate routine and event branches;
- organize local/certified/mail/home-screening routes by evidence function;
- maintain persistent sample/result context;
- guide confirmation/retest decisions;
- route to source/well correction or a defined treatment job without prescribing the product;
- schedule recurring/event verification;
- preserve the learner's household history so future decisions start from evidence instead of memory.

That is sufficient for paid-depth Product Design exploration. **If the product becomes mostly reading/cards, the paid-value test fails.**

## Pricing direction

No public or owner-approved price is established at this stage.

Current comparable economics checked 2026-08-23 show a wide spread:
- Florida DOH describes local routine testing as usually about **$20–$30 per sample**;
- nearby Alachua currently lists microbiological analysis at **$25 per bottle**;
- current commercial mail-to-lab listings show well panels around **$209, $399 and $1,125**, depending on scope.

This spread supports the value of better **test selection and route comparison**, but laboratory prices do not directly set the value of an educational/product tool. Pricing must wait for completed architecture and the actual learner work/output.

## Product Design gate conclusion

Product Opportunity Research is sufficient to advance from `PRODUCT_OPPORTUNITY_RESEARCH` to `PRODUCT_WORKING`.

Next Product Design work must create the complete integrated experience, including at minimum:
- product architecture;
- learner-facing manuscript/content;
- My Well Test Plan specification;
- Test Route Comparator;
- result anatomy / interpretation workspace;
- routine/event/local-risk branch logic;
- sample journey and result-to-next-action visuals;
- persistent navigation/state contract;
- responsible verification activities;
- learner signal plan;
- preservation check;
- visual production brief;
- pricing/package recommendation;
- Product QA.

No Product Review, Visual Production, public pricing, release, sale, publication or deployment is authorized by this scorecard.
