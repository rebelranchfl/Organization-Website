This project supports:

- historical-knowledge recovery (showing this is an old, cross-cultural idea, not a 1990s invention);

- responsible-rebellion framing: questioning an over-marketed claim while still respecting and teaching the real, evidence-backed skill underneath it.

## Boundaries / conflict check
No conflict was found between this idea and the governing documents. One hard boundary was identified and is treated as binding for every later stage of this project, not just Research: the RRA Concept & Direction document explicitly lists "professional tax, legal, financial, mental-health, or medical services" among things RRA must not present itself as offering (§12). This project teaches the general, evidence-backed psychological science of emotional intelligence — it must never be framed, marketed, or built as therapy, counseling, diagnosis, or a substitute for licensed mental-health care. Any point where content could reasonably surface real emotional distress in a learner should carry a specific, targeted note pointing to professional support — not a blanket disclaimer, and not silence.

## Governing evidence rules applied
> Authority does not replace evidence. Proximity to the source, transparency, corroboration, and relevance matter more than institutional prestige.

> Evidence determines what RRA may teach. Uncertainty determines what RRA may say about the learner's specific situation. Uncertainty does not prohibit education.

> Do not overclaim. Do not under-teach.

Applied directly in this project: Goleman's popular claim is taught as the popular, influential starting point it genuinely is, while being explicitly checked against, and corrected by, independent peer-reviewed evidence — rather than either uncritically repeating the inflated claim or dismissing the topic as unscientific.
