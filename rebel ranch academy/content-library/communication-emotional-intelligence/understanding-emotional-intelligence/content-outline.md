# Content Outline — RRA-2026-0011: What Is Emotional Intelligence?

1. **Hook / real moment** — a recognizable scene of reacting badly to a feeling vs. handling it well.
2. **Core explanation** — the four abilities (Perceive, Use, Understand, Manage), plain language, from Salovey & Mayer via the four-branch model.
3. **IQ vs. EQ** — defines IQ on its own terms (Wechsler deviation IQ, mean 100/SD 15) before contrasting it with EQ; book-smart vs. people-smart with two concrete, contrasting examples. Added per owner review 2026-09-14 — the original outline contrasted EQ against IQ without ever defining IQ.
4. **Where this came from** — Thorndike (1920) → Gardner (1983) → Salovey & Mayer (1990), positioned as UNDERSTAND/EXPLORE, with Diagram 3 (historical lineage timeline, queued 2026-09-14 third owner review pass — this page was flagged as too thin: one paragraph, no graphic, no interactivity).
5. **Myth check** — Goleman's popular "EQ beats IQ" version vs. the narrower, better-evidenced academic version; Locke's objection; the honest middle ground (Joseph & Newman meta-analysis). This is the COMPARE step.
6. **Diagram 1** — the four abilities, visual.
7. **The one technique that's actually been tested** — reappraisal vs. suppression (Gross, 1998), with Diagram 2 (comparison visual).
8. **Try It** — "Match & Check" activity: matching exercise (ability → definition) plus a scenario checkbox exercise (reappraisal vs. suppression), APPLY step. Redesigned per owner review 2026-09-14 — replaced free-text paragraph answers, which owner rejected as impractical for this audience.
9. **Take It Further** — RULER structure for families/classrooms wanting a repeatable framework, with Diagram 4 (RULER five-step diagram, queued 2026-09-14) and a matching exercise (step → meaning, built 2026-09-14) — this page was flagged as too thin: one paragraph, no graphic, no interactivity.
10. **REBEL RANCH PRINCIPLE** — personal, family, communication, work, money/leadership connections, and where the skill stops applying. Renamed from "TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE" per owner decision 2026-09-14 (doctrine-wide rename, see ACADEMY-CONTENT-PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md Section 7). Revised again 2026-09-14 (second owner review pass) to open with a plain explanation of what "Rebel Ranch Principle" means for a first-time reader, to explain the actual mechanism behind each connection instead of asserting an outcome, and to say "emotion" instead of the vaguer "signal" except where genuinely generalizing beyond emotion.
11. **Quick Quiz** — short multiple-choice/checkbox check of the lesson's core claims (four abilities, IQ vs. EQ, reappraisal vs. suppression). Added per owner review 2026-09-14.
12. **One thing to try** — learner names one real upcoming moment they'll apply reappraisal to (the CREATE step). Split out from the old combined "Before you go" section per owner review 2026-09-14 (second pass) — that section had mixed a safety disclaimer, this prompt, and the source table together under one confusing label.
13. **Glossary** — every defined term used anywhere in the lesson, consolidated at the end. Added per owner review 2026-09-14 (second pass); replaces the first pass's per-section glossary boxes, which owner clarified was not what she meant by "glossary."
