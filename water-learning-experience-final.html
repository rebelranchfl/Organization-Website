One Source. Different Jobs.

Choose treatment from the source and the intended use, not from a one-size-fits-all recipe.

Drink / CookTest the source and use a verified potable-water treatment path.
AnimalsMatch water quality, supply and distribution to the animal and setting.
IrrigationMatch plant, soil, flow and storage needs without unnecessary treatment.
EmergencyPlan protected storage, rotation and a verified drinking-water branch before the emergency.

Build by Function

1. Know the source.
Identify what can enter the water before it reaches the system.
2. Define the job.
Drinking, animals, irrigation and emergency use have different requirements.
3. Match treatment.
Use each media or treatment step because it performs a needed function.
4. Maintain + verify.
Clean, replace media, test when appropriate, and confirm the system still performs its job.
