## Owner-approved direction being locked in

Expand the Product Design Agent and product-phase workflow so recommendations are evidence-driven, differentiated, historically informed, globally/resource-spectrum aware, implementation-oriented, and valuable at both free and paid levels.

Required additions:

- historical/pattern analysis across years and centuries;
- environmental and resource-spectrum comparison from high-resource/high-income settings through constrained/low-resource settings;
- modernization tradeoff analysis: comfort, safety, scale, outsourcing, lost capability, dependency, resilience, and new problems;
- audience/problem demand research;
- market/comparable research focused on gaps, complaints, unresolved questions, and unmet needs rather than imitation;
- mandatory differentiation/opportunity design that produces concrete opportunities, not observations;
- implementation pathways for realistic use cases (household, homestead, emergency/preparedness, animals/agriculture, etc. when appropriate);
- cost, savings, tradeoff, maintenance, time, and capability analysis when claims can be supported;
- Knowledge Compression Value: preserve depth while reducing the learner's time, confusion, cross-referencing burden, and unnecessary technical friction;
- free products receive the same research/differentiation rigor as paid products;
- free is a pricing decision, not a quality classification;
- where comparables support it, RRA may communicate the market value of knowledge given away free without inventing claims;
- Product Recommendation Scorecard must cite its evidence and explain why RRA should be chosen over real alternatives;
- product architecture and monetization/mission strategy must be based on the preceding evidence layers;
- required analysis of what changes if the learner actually implements the knowledge.
