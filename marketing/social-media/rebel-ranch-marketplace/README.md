# Rebel Ranch Local — Marketplace Marketing Workspace

This folder keeps Rebel Ranch Local / Marketplace brand assets, launch visuals, messaging context, and campaign planning separate from general Rebel Ranch Ministries social media.

## Product structure

**Rebel Ranch Local** is the local-commerce umbrella.

- **Marketplace** — local food and farm products such as meat, produce, raw milk, honey, canned goods, baked goods, food trucks, and similar local food offerings.
- **Goods** — handmade/local products such as candles, soap, tinctures, crafts, and other legitimate locally made goods.
- **Services** — local tradespeople and service providers such as repair, lawn care, cleaning, skilled trades, and other small local operators.

The public-facing purpose is simple: make it easier for people to find what their own community already grows, makes, sells, and does.

## Core product purpose

Rebel Ranch Local was designed to solve a visibility and access problem:

- Facebook and social posts disappear.
- Sellers met at festivals or roadside stands are hard to find again.
- Local services are scattered across word-of-mouth, social pages, search results, and personal contacts.
- Small operators compete for attention against large businesses with much larger marketing budgets.
- Buyers often want to support local but do not have one dependable place to start.

Rebel Ranch Local gives local sellers/providers a fairer place to be found and gives buyers one easier place to find local food, goods, and services.

## Seller model

- Free to join / no marketplace membership fee.
- No Rebel Ranch commission on sales.
- Seller/provider deals directly with the buyer.
- Seller controls accepted payment methods and pickup/delivery/shipping arrangements.
- Participation is intended for genuine local growers, makers, sole proprietors, service providers, tradespeople, side businesses, young entrepreneurs with appropriate parent/guardian involvement, and very small teams aligned with the mission.

## Buyer model

Browse/search by need, find a local seller or provider, view the listing/storefront, and connect directly. The goal is to replace hunting across scattered posts and directories with a simple local starting point.

## Rebel Ranch Ministries role

Rebel Ranch Local is built by **Rebel Ranch Ministries** as part of the larger mission of reconnecting local people, useful skills, products, services, and community resources. Public marketing should say **Rebel Ranch Ministries**, not shorten the organization name to "Rebel Ranch" when describing who built or supports the program.

## Brand / visual direction

- Master identity: realistic imagery, not generic vector/cartoon farm art.
- Primary composition: three clearly separated visual sections for Marketplace, Goods, and Services.
- Marketplace visual cues include food/produce, raw milk, honey, canned goods, and meat.
- Goods visual cues include handmade candle, tincture, and tagged soap.
- Services visual cue uses a trade pouch with distinct tools and visible `TRADES` marking.
- Local map-pin element is part of the identity; do not add extra decorative imagery inside the pin unless specifically approved.
- `REBEL RANCH` type uses only **light/minimal distress**. Do not progressively increase the distressed treatment.
- `LOCAL` and supporting text should remain clean and highly readable.
- Current category line: **MARKETPLACE • GOODS • SERVICES**.
- Do not change approved logo elements casually. Derivative assets must be built from the same locked master identity rather than independently regenerated variations.

## Campaign content scenarios

The launch program currently has eight core scenarios:

1. What it is / overall benefit.
2. The problem that caused it to be built.
3. Why sellers should join.
4. Why buyers should use it.
5. Why it helps the community.
6. Community input — ask what people want, what is missing, and what they would use.
7. Why Rebel Ranch Ministries built it / how it fits the mission.
8. How to join, shop, search, and use the app.

Supporting launch content should also include real website/app screenshots, category-specific Marketplace/Goods/Services reels, real seller/listing examples as they become available, and simple onboarding/how-to visuals.

## Marketing rule

Do not make every reel repeat the same information or the same CTA. Each visual/reel gets **one primary job** and **one primary CTA**: awareness, comment, share, follow, seller signup, buyer use, or community feedback.
