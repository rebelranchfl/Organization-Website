# Flag — Safety Boundary Over-Calibration (documented per owner request, 2026-09-14)

Status: **flagged for owner attention, not resolved.** This is a record of a real, recurring problem the owner named directly, kept here so it doesn't get lost or silently "fixed" by one agent's unilateral edit to governing doctrine.

## What the owner said (paraphrased from the 2026-09-14 second review pass on RRA-2026-0011)

While reviewing the rendered lesson, the owner objected to a one-line safety disclaimer ("this lesson describes a psychological concept, not a treatment... calls for a qualified professional, not a worksheet") on a purely educational, non-diagnostic emotional-intelligence lesson. Her point, generalized beyond this one project: the Academy's safety mechanisms had previously become so restrictive that the pipeline "couldn't function" — content and process got treated as a safety issue by default, even when nothing about the actual content warranted it. She asked that this be documented as an issue, and that the disclaimer be dropped from this specific project.

## What was done

The disclaimer was removed from RRA-2026-0011 (`master-content.md`, `eq-lesson-preview.html`) per her direct instruction — see `qa-review.md` and `content-outline.md` revision notes on that project for the specifics.

## What was deliberately NOT done

`ACADEMY-FINAL-PRODUCT-ACCEPTANCE-STANDARD.md`'s "Safety and boundaries" section (which asks for "a proportional, single, plain-language boundary note... rather than a wall of disclaimers") was **not** edited. That standard already tries to be proportional rather than maximal, and the actual right calibration — when a boundary note is warranted at all vs. when it's reflexive over-caution — is a real policy judgment call with legitimate liability considerations (the Academy does serve minors and families), not something to resolve by silently rewriting doctrine inside a single content project's revision pass.

## Open question for the owner

Should `ACADEMY-FINAL-PRODUCT-ACCEPTANCE-STANDARD.md` be revised so that a safety/boundary note is judged case-by-case against whether the specific content is diagnostic/clinical/sensitive-topic-adjacent, rather than defaulted onto every project regardless of subject? If so, that's a doctrine-level edit for a future session to make deliberately, with the owner's explicit sign-off on the new wording — the same pattern already used this session for the "REBEL RANCH PRINCIPLE" rename.
