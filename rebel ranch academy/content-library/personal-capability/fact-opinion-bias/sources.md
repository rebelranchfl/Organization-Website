## Research-agent source judgment

No source is being used because of institutional prestige alone. The source set combines primary empirical studies, scholarly synthesis, a primary historical text and one basic educational reference used only to show the common simplified fact/opinion teaching model.

The high-confidence conclusions are supported by multiple independent lines of evidence:

- people can make systematic judgment errors;
- prior beliefs and desired conclusions can shape evidence evaluation;
- people often recognize bias more readily in others than in themselves;
- repetition can increase perceived truth;
- deliberately shifting attention to accuracy and considering opposing possibilities can improve some judgments.

The material must **not** teach that detecting bias automatically disproves a claim, that disagreement proves bias, or that opinions are worthless. Bias is a reason to inspect the reasoning process more carefully, not a shortcut for dismissing another person.
