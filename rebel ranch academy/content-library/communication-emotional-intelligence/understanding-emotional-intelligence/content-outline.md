# Content Outline — RRA-2026-0011: What Is Emotional Intelligence?

1. **Hook / real moment** — a recognizable scene of reacting badly to a feeling vs. handling it well.
2. **Core explanation** — the four abilities (Perceive, Use, Understand, Manage), plain language, from Salovey & Mayer via the four-branch model.
3. **Where this came from** — Thorndike (1920) → Gardner (1983) → Salovey & Mayer (1990), one paragraph, positioned as UNDERSTAND/EXPLORE.
4. **Myth check** — Goleman's popular "EQ beats IQ" version vs. the narrower, better-evidenced academic version; Locke's objection; the honest middle ground (Joseph & Newman meta-analysis). This is the COMPARE step.
5. **Diagram 1** — the four abilities, visual.
6. **The one technique that's actually been tested** — reappraisal vs. suppression (Gross, 1998), with Diagram 2 (comparison visual).
7. **Try It** — "Name It to Tame It" activity walkthrough (APPLY).
8. **Take It Further** — RULER structure for families/classrooms wanting a repeatable framework.
9. **TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE** — personal, family, communication, work, money/leadership connections, and where the comparison stops being valid.
10. **What this is not** — one-line, proportional safety note: not therapy/diagnosis.
11. **Close / CREATE step** — learner names one real upcoming moment they'll apply reappraisal to.
