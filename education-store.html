Rebel Ranch Ministries may offer items already in stock, donated goods that have been inspected and described, and new items sourced after an order request. Product availability, condition, shipping, pickup, and final price are confirmed before payment.

PEP families: Product eligibility and reimbursement decisions are made by the scholarship funding organization under the current purchasing guide. We provide clear retail documentation; families remain responsible for confirming eligibility and submitting their request.

Documentation included

  • Rebel Ranch Ministries business name
  • Complete purchase date
  • Specific item description
  • Quantity and item price
  • Subtotal, tax, shipping, fees, discounts, and total
  • Payment method and paid-in-full status
  • Condition noted for donated or previously owned inventory
