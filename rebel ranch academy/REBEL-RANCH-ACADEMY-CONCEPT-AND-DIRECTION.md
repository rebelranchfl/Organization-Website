# Rebel Ranch Academy — Concept and Direction

**Status:** Owner-approved concept and program direction  
**Approved:** 2026-08-20  
**Reconciled with current RRM program structure:** 2026-09-07  
**Program:** Rebel Ranch Academy (RRA)  
**Parent program:** Rebel Ranch Ministries (RRM)  
**Parent organization:** Faith, Family & Nature Church, Inc.

## 1. Purpose of this document

This document gives the owner, staff, contractors, and AI agents one shared understanding of Rebel Ranch Academy before program, curriculum, marketing, website, or visual work begins.

Read this document together with:

1. the repository `AGENTS.md`;
2. `docs/rebel-ranch-ecosystem-charter.md`; and
3. any later owner-approved RRA visual, curriculum, or offer standards.

If an older note, prototype, page, or draft conflicts with this direction, stop and raise the conflict. Do not silently choose one.

## 2. Core concept

Rebel Ranch Academy is the practical education program of Rebel Ranch Ministries for children, teens, and adults.

RRA teaches real-life skills that are often missing from traditional education. It helps people become more capable, confident, responsible, accountable, independent, resilient, and strong.

RRA is not a conventional school, a classroom-only brand, or an agriculture-only program. It combines learning with real-world action and application.

### Core outcome

> Growing capable, confident, responsible people through real-life skills.

### Supporting Rebel Ranch language

- Real People. Real Skills. Real Community.
- Faith, Family & Freedom.
- Responsible rebellion.
- Learn it. Use it. Live it.

The last line is a working expression, not a locked public tagline, until separately approved by the owner.

## 3. Responsible rebellion

RRA challenges the idea that conventional education is the only legitimate path to knowledge or capability.

The Academy supports people who take ownership of their:

- education;
- decisions;
- work;
- finances;
- communication;
- relationships;
- families;
- food and resources; and
- future.

This rebellion is disciplined and responsible. RRA is not rude, reckless, disrespectful, chaotic, or anti-knowledge. It builds people who are respectable, responsible, accountable, confident, independent, and strong.

The tone should communicate:

- we question systems that are not serving people;
- we learn what other people may not teach;
- we take responsibility for what happens next;
- we respect useful knowledge and proven skill;
- we do not wait for permission to become capable; and
- we do not use rebellion as an excuse to avoid accountability.

## 4. Place in the Rebel Ranch ecosystem

Rebel Ranch Ministries is the community resource hub. Rebel Ranch Academy is one program offered through that hub.

RRM may connect people to multiple programs, but the programs remain distinct.

### Creation Station

Creation Station supports creative exploration, guided projects, creator development, portfolios, and related opportunities. It is a separate RRM program.

Creation Station participants may be directed to RRA when they need deeper personal, business, financial, communication, leadership, or practical education.

Creation Station is not a department of RRA.

### Business Freedom

Business Freedom helps a sole owner or small business address an immediate operational problem.

RRA provides longer-term education that helps people understand and build business capability.

A simple distinction:

- **RRA:** learn the knowledge and skills.
- **Business Freedom:** receive practical help with a current business problem.

Do not combine these offers or imply that an Academy class includes Business Freedom services.

### Rebel Ranch Local / Marketplace

Rebel Ranch Local is RRM's broader local-economic and community-commerce program. Marketplace is one component of RRL.

Marketplace participants may be directed to RRA for education involving pricing, money, customer relationships, communication, planning, operations, resilience, or leadership.

Marketplace participation is not Academy enrollment, and Academy participation is not Marketplace approval.

### Roots, Boots & Animal Poops

Roots, Boots & Animal Poops (RBAP) is a separate RRM program focused on agriculture, animal stewardship, homesteading, long-term food production, food security, sustainability, and related practical capability.

RRA may teach, organize, package, expand, or distribute education developed from RBAP subjects and real-world experience, but **RBAP is not a department, learning area, or named program inside Rebel Ranch Academy.**

RRA and RBAP may work together without losing their separate program identities.

### 3P Help Me

3P Help Me is separate from Rebel Ranch Ministries and is not an RRA program or brand.

Do not place 3P branding on RRA programs, classes, pages, materials, or credentials unless the owner separately authorizes a clearly described relationship.

## 5. Academy organization

RRA should be organized as one Academy with several learning areas. The subjects do not need to look or sound identical. The shared outcome—capable people with practical knowledge—connects them.

### Required naming structure

For programs and series that belong to RRA, use this hierarchy:

```text
Rebel Ranch Ministries
└── Rebel Ranch Academy
    └── Learning Area
        └── Named RRA Program or Series
            └── Individual Class, Session, Course, or Resource
```

A separate RRM program may contribute subject matter or real-world knowledge to RRA without being nested under RRA. For example, agriculture education may draw from RBAP while RBAP remains a separate RRM program.

Do not turn every learning area into an unrelated brand. RRA-owned named programs may have supporting identities, logos, and campaign styles, but they must clearly identify themselves as part of Rebel Ranch Academy.

## 7. RRA relationship with Roots, Boots & Animal Poops

RBAP and RRA are sibling programs under Rebel Ranch Ministries.

RBAP develops and practices agriculture, animal stewardship, homesteading, food-production, sustainability, and related real-world capability. RRA may turn appropriate knowledge from those areas into broader educational experiences, courses, resources, or learning paths.

A simple distinction is:

- **RBAP:** the agriculture, animal-stewardship, homesteading, and food-production program and its real-world practice.
- **RRA:** the broader education program that may teach applicable agriculture and sustainability knowledge alongside many other subjects.

When RRA uses RBAP material, branding must remain accurate. Do not present RBAP as “A Rebel Ranch Academy Program.” Do not remove RBAP's own program identity. If a joint educational product is created later, its exact co-branding and ownership must be approved before publication.

The existing RRA and RBAP logo files in the repository must be used as supplied when their respective brands are represented. Do not redraw, recreate, or substitute either logo.

## 8. Audience paths

RRA serves more than one market. The website and marketing should let people enter through the need that matters to them rather than forcing every subject into one message.

### Parents and homeschool families

Primary message:

> Build capable kids with skills they will use for life.

Relevant areas may include personal strength, communication, emotional intelligence, money, responsibility, leadership, sustainability, agriculture, and age-appropriate business knowledge.

Parent or guardian control and privacy must be preserved for minors.

### Teens and young adults

Primary message:

> Learn the real-life skills adulthood will expect you to have.

Relevant areas may include confidence, communication, work readiness, money, business, decision-making, leadership, and independence.

### Adults

Primary message:

> Learn the skills many people spend a lifetime figuring out.

Relevant areas may include relationships, communication, emotional intelligence, money, business, personal strength, resilience, family, and leadership.

### Sole owners and small-business operators

Primary message:

> Learn how to build and operate a business that works.

Relevant areas may include planning, pricing, customer relationships, operations, money, process improvement, leadership, quality, and Six Sigma.

This is education through RRA, not immediate Business Freedom service work.

### Homesteaders and agriculture learners

Primary message:

> Learn where real food comes from—and how to grow, raise, and steward it responsibly.

Relevant RRA education may include food systems, soil, water, animal stewardship, land care, resourcefulness, and sustainability. RRA may connect learners to RBAP where the separate RBAP program is the more appropriate destination.

## 9. Marketing direction

Do not advertise every Academy subject in one message.

Each campaign should contain:

1. one audience;
2. one problem, aspiration, or learning need;
3. one RRA learning area or approved RRA program/series;
4. one clear benefit;
5. one real clickable action; and
6. visible Rebel Ranch Academy identification.

Examples of focused campaign directions:

- capable kids for parents and homeschoolers;
- real-life readiness for teens;
- skills adults wish they had learned earlier;
- communication for difficult people and hard conversations;
- business knowledge for sole owners;
- money and tax understanding;
- sustainability or agriculture education when offered through RRA; and
- confidence, accountability, and responsible independence.

If a campaign is actually promoting RBAP, follow RBAP's own program and brand controls rather than presenting the campaign as an RRA program campaign.

Do not reduce RRA to ranching or agriculture because “Ranch” appears in the name. Agriculture is one learning area inside a much broader Academy.

Do not make every RRA campaign faith-centered. Faith, Family & Freedom is part of the Rebel Ranch identity, but individual education messages should lead with the audience's real need and the practical value.

## 10. Website direction

The intended website path is:

1. the RRM website introduces Rebel Ranch Ministries programs;
2. the interactive RRA Program Hub at `https://academy.rebelranchministries.org` is the live public Academy destination;
3. the existing `academy.html` remains unchanged and on hold for a later content review;
4. visitors choose an audience, need, or learning area;
5. the hub presents relevant programs, classes, or interest paths; and
6. focused program pages or forms collect interest when an offer is not yet open.

The interactive hub prototype may be used as a functional starting point. Its useful interactions may include:

- audience or subject filtering;
- program exploration;
- lesson or class details;
- a device-local learning plan; and
- device-local progress tracking.

Prototype labels, program structures, copy, colors, typography, logos, navigation, or footer are not automatically approved merely because they exist in the prototype. They must be reconciled with this document and the repository rules.

No change to `academy.html` is authorized by this document alone.

## 11. Visual direction

RRA should have its own recognizable visual identity while clearly remaining a program of Rebel Ranch Ministries.

The intended character is:

- prestigious;
- polished;
- credible;
- established;
- strong;
- grounded;
- respectable;
- responsibly rebellious; and
- accessible to both younger and adult learners.

It should feel like Academy-level credibility without institutional conformity.

### Visual foundation

Use the existing approved RRA logo as the starting point. Do not create a substitute wordmark or redraw the emblem.

The approved public-hub color direction is:

- midnight and deep navy for strength and Academy-level credibility;
- antique gold for accents and important actions;
- warm cream and paper for readable learning surfaces;
- charcoal and navy text on every cream or paper surface; and
- limited visual connections back to RRM where needed.

The approved implementation is documented in the Program Hub source, especially `app/globals.css`. Gold or pale yellow text must not be used on cream or other light surfaces. The polished public hub uses Georgia for formal headings and Arial/Helvetica for clear body content.

Do not use visual rebellion as an excuse for distressed clutter, graffiti, chaos, aggression, or disrespect.

### Interactive-shape rule

A full pill or capsule shape is reserved for a real clickable link or control.

Do not use pill-shaped tags, labels, badges, statuses, category markers, or decorative text containers. If an element does not click, it must not look like a pill-shaped button.

## 12. Content and offer safeguards

Until separately approved, do not invent or present as available:

- specific classes or courses;
- prices;
- subscriptions;
- schedules;
- certificates;
- credentials;
- age requirements;
- instructors;
- enrollment limits;
- guaranteed outcomes;
- income or profit promises;
- professional tax, legal, financial, mental-health, or medical services; or
- account-synced progress.

Interest collection must clearly distinguish planned subjects from currently open programs.

Any youth experience must preserve parent or guardian control, age-appropriate language, and privacy.

## 14. Direction summary

Rebel Ranch Academy is one Academy with multiple practical learning areas.

Its subjects may range from emotional intelligence and communication to business, money, taxes, Six Sigma, sustainability, agriculture, and animal stewardship. They belong together because every area builds real-life capability.

RRA should market each need to the correct audience instead of trying to explain the entire Academy in one advertisement.

Separate RRM programs such as Roots, Boots & Animal Poops may contribute knowledge, real-world practice, media, or educational subject matter to RRA without becoming nested under the Academy. Each program keeps its own identity and controls.

The result should be a credible, polished, grounded Academy that helps children and adults become capable, confident, responsible, accountable, independent, resilient, and strong.
