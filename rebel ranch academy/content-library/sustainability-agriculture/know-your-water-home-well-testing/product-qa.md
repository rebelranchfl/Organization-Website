## Evidence-boundary check

The design does **not** introduce unsupported claims that:
- one universal national testing panel applies to every private well;
- a consumer home kit is equivalent to certified laboratory analysis for high-consequence drinking-water decisions;
- Florida-specific recommendations apply everywhere;
- one sample procedure applies to every analyte/lab;
- specialized PFAS/radionuclide/arsenic/pesticide/VOC testing logic is universal;
- animal or irrigation suitability thresholds are established by the current approved evidence;
- treatment can be selected before the treatment job is identified.

## Multi-use check

PASS. The learner may select more than one real use. The system does not force a single dominant use merely for interface simplicity. Where approved evidence does not define use-specific thresholds, the product preserves the use and explicitly routes to relevant evidence/local guidance rather than inventing certainty.

## Testing check

PASS. Testing appears in three roles:
1. build/choose the evidence plan;
2. interpret the measurement in context;
3. verify a correction or treatment-performance question.

The product explicitly teaches what can be understood/prepared before a result and what learner-specific decisions should wait.

## Navigation/state check

PASS at specification level. Mandatory Visual Production acceptance tests:
- no dead links/404s;
- exact return from every depth control;
- answers and selected uses persist;
- adding/removing events updates branches without deleting historical records;
- result records retain units/sample point/benchmark/source in summaries;
- My Well Test Plan updates when earlier answers change.

Rendered implementation must be tested later; this Product QA does not claim code exists yet.
