# Change Description — Marketing Documentation Boundary

Date: 2026-09-06

Owner-authorized repository-control update.

Target file: `/AGENTS.md`

Purpose: prevent repository-wide marketing documentation from absorbing or duplicating program-specific marketing documentation.

Approved boundary:

- Repository-level Marketing governs shared marketing systems, rules, workflows, approvals, publishing processes, shared analytics/reporting, cross-program coordination, and reusable methods that apply across multiple RRM programs.
- Program-level Marketing governs that program's specific audiences, campaigns, offers, messaging, assets, launch plans, program-specific CTAs, and execution.
- If a marketing rule/process applies across multiple programs, its authoritative home is repository-level Marketing.
- If marketing material only makes sense for one program, its authoritative home is that program's ecosystem.
- Program marketing should reference shared repo-level marketing rules/workflows rather than copy them.
- Repository-level Marketing must not redefine or override a program's approved brand or program-specific marketing decisions.
- Existing files are to be classified by their actual contents before moving or consolidating them.

No marketing folders are being created or moved in this change. No marketing copy, campaign, website, deployment, program brand, or live behavior is being changed.
