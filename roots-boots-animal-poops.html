Instructor Bios

Animal Care & Husbandry

Brooklyn Ritchie

Brooklyn's experience in animal care began approximately 22 years ago at Safe Harbor Animal Sanctuary & Hospital in Jupiter, Florida, where she managed operations involving animal rescue, boarding, and veterinary care. The nonprofit facility cared for animals in need, and her work brought her into contact with dogs, cats, rabbits, ferrets, and other rescued animals.

That early experience established a lasting interest in animal welfare, responsible care, and the operational demands of caring for animals with different needs. Since 2023, she has extended that experience into hands-on agricultural work at Rebel Ranch, raising and caring for pigs, rabbits, chickens, and ducks and developing practical feeding, habitat, and foraging systems.

Brooklyn's instruction combines experience in rescue operations with everyday livestock management to explore animal behavior, nutrition, responsible handling, appropriate habitats, and the relationship between animal care and natural systems.

Growing Food: Soil, Seeds & Plants

Brooklyn Ritchie

Brooklyn's experience growing food began during the COVID-19 pandemic, when concerns about food availability led her family to develop their own growing practices.

That experience grew into Permaculture Queen, an educational platform through which she shared gardening knowledge and actively helped others cultivate food, establish pollinator habitats, and understand practical approaches to sustainable growing.

She also helped initiate a garden program at Thelma B. Pittman Preschool, connecting food cultivation with early childhood learning and on-site food preparation.

Today, she continues applying these principles at Rebel Ranch through gardening, food production, and soil improvement.

Soil, Water & Natural Systems

Brooklyn Ritchie

Brooklyn combines practical agricultural experience with a professional background in systems engineering, scientific problem-solving, and continuous improvement.

At Rebel Ranch, her work includes soil improvement, livestock-based nutrient cycles, worm farming, natural resource management, and developing feeding and grazing practices that support the land.

Her earlier educational work through Permaculture Queen and Thelma B. Pittman Preschool also connects growing food with an understanding of soil, plants, and ecological relationships.

Brooklyn approaches natural systems through observation and investigation, helping students understand how soil, water, animals, and plants influence one another.

Farm to Table

Brooklyn Ritchie

Brooklyn's background in food production and agricultural education developed through her family's efforts to grow food during the COVID-19 pandemic, her educational outreach through Permaculture Queen, and her subsequent hands-on agricultural work at Rebel Ranch.

At Thelma B. Pittman Preschool, she helped initiate a gardening program that connected food cultivation with learning and an existing on-site kitchen where meals were prepared.

Her agricultural experience also includes raising livestock and developing systems that support food production.

Brooklyn brings these experiences together to help students understand the connections among animal care, gardening, soil fertility, responsible food production, and the journey from farm to table.

Hydroponics

Brooklyn Ritchie

Brooklyn's practical experience with hydroponics began during the COVID-19 pandemic and continued through her Permaculture Queen educational work and ongoing agricultural programs at Rebel Ranch Ministries. Her work connects soilless food cultivation with resource management and the design, operation, and observation of growing systems.

She also brings a technical foundation developed through electronics studies and work on complex systems at Lockheed Martin and Johnson & Johnson, alongside experience teaching others to grow food and develop practical agricultural skills.

Brooklyn's instruction connects plant nutrition, nutrient solutions, water circulation, system assembly, monitoring, and troubleshooting with practical understanding of how hydroponic systems function.

Aquaponics

Brooklyn Ritchie

Brooklyn's experience with aquaponics began during the COVID-19 pandemic and continued through Permaculture Queen and the agricultural work of Rebel Ranch Ministries. Her approach combines practical growing experience with an understanding of how biological and mechanical systems operate together.

Her professional background includes electronics, technical systems, systems engineering, and process improvement at Lockheed Martin and Johnson & Johnson. She has also developed practical educational programs focused on growing food and understanding natural systems.

In aquaponics instruction, Brooklyn connects fish and plant biology, nutrient cycling, biofiltration, water circulation, system design, and monitoring to the operation of an integrated food-growing system.
