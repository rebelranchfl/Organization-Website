# Rebel Ranch Ministries Outreach Archive

## Purpose

This file tracks every outreach message actually sent, so the agent does
not contact the same business, chamber, or organization repeatedly, and so
Brooke has a record of who has been reached out to and what happened.

This is the source of truth for outreach history. `outreach-queue.md`
remains the only working queue — approved-but-unsent entries stay there,
not here.

The agent alone maintains this archive after an authorized message is
sent. Brooke only approves, rejects, corrects, or supplies judgment when
needed.

---

## Reuse Rules

Do not contact the same business/org/event organizer again within a short
window unless they replied and a follow-up is warranted.

Do not send the same generic message to multiple targets without
adjusting it to that specific business, org, or event.

If a target does not respond, record that and wait for Brooke's judgment
on whether/when to follow up — do not auto-repeat outreach.
