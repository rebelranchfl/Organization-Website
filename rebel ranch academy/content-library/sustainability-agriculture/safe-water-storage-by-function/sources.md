## Evidence conflicts / boundaries
- CDC emergency rotation guidance is a practical emergency-preparedness rule, not a universal microbiological expiration date for every stored-water configuration.
- Human potable-water storage rules should not be copied automatically into livestock or irrigation standards; intended use changes the consequence and verification target.
- The South Africa container study shows interaction among water quality, surface material and time, not a universal conclusion that galvanized steel is safer than polyethylene in every system.
- "Food-grade" or "potable-water" suitability should be verified for the actual container and intended use; prior toxic-chemical service is a hard exclusion for potable storage in CDC/WHO guidance.
