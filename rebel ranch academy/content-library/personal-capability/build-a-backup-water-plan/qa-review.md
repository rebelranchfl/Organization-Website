## Material evidence boundaries to preserve downstream

1. No single storage quantity is universal. CDC, Florida and WHO/Sphere values answer different duration/use scopes.
2. Boiling/disinfection is not a universal treatment for chemical/toxic contamination.
3. Alternate sources require source/event/intended-use evaluation; physical availability is not proof of potability.
4. Flooded or contaminated wells may require disinfection and testing before return to drinking use.
5. Historical systems demonstrate practice and transferable system logic, not modern safety by age alone.
