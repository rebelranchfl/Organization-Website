## Source-use conclusions
1. **Traditional is a history category, not an efficacy rating.** Historical use and modern mechanism are recorded separately.
2. **Africa is not one practice.** Revision 2 uses named West African studies rather than continental generalization.
3. **Copper has real antimicrobial evidence, narrowly stated.** It is not a universal purifier or health tonic.
4. **Frequency can affect water physically.** Acoustic manipulation and molecular spectroscopy establish that. They do not establish every popular claim about “structured” or “energized” water.
5. **The Moon/human question is not dismissed.** Positive human sleep studies, non-replication and new 2026 gravimetric evidence are all included.
6. **Correlation, mechanism and health benefit are separate claims.** The final material must keep them separate.
7. Existing drinking-water safety boundaries remain: no learner experiment output is represented as potable without a separately validated process.
