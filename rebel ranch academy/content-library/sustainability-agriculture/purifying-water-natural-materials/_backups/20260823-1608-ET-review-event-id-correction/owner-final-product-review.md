## Owner direction

The owner found the product still felt too much like “you should drink clean water to be safe” instead of the deeper understanding-and-education experience required for Rebel Ranch Academy.

The owner directed the Product Design rebuild to center on:

- building and understanding filtered-water systems;
- what different layers, materials and components actually do;
- how water systems worked through history and in different regions;
- practical `if you have this on hand, try this design/material` substitutions where evidence supports them;
- teaching function and system thinking rather than merely recommending a safe outcome;
- keeping testing necessary and recommended while explaining what it changes, affordable/free/local routes when verified, and home-versus-lab limits;
- never using a missing learner-specific test as a blanket reason to stop teaching evidence-backed general facts, functions and systems.

This decision preserves the approved Revision 3 + UV research/evidence foundation. It does not authorize evidence replacement or weaker safety boundaries.
