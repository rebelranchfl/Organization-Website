# Business Freedom — Agent Entry Point

**Status:** Current Business Freedom agent routing control  
**Program:** Rebel Ranch Ministries → Business Freedom  
**Applies to:** Any AI agent or operator working on Business Freedom strategy, offers, public pages, marketing, systems, delivery documentation, or related Marketplace funnels.

This file does not replace the repository-wide `AGENTS.md`. It tells an agent what Business Freedom-specific authorities to read and how to route Business Freedom work without creating duplicate rules.

## 1. Read in this order

Before changing or documenting Business Freedom work:

1. Read repository root `AGENTS.md`.
2. Read `docs/rebel-ranch-ecosystem-charter.md`.
3. Read this file.
4. Read `business-freedom-operations.md` for program purpose, boundaries, current service map, audience, claims limits, Business Freedom/3P separation, and current priorities.
5. Read `README.md` for the current index and work order.
6. Read `brand.md` when visual, page, marketing, or experience work is involved.
7. Read the applicable record under `pages/` when working on a specific Business Freedom service, offer, or public page.
8. Verify the current repository implementation and public/live state before making a status claim.

If two current authorities conflict, stop and surface the conflict to the owner in plain language. Do not choose one silently.

## 2. What Business Freedom is

Business Freedom is a Rebel Ranch Ministries program for sole operators and owner-operated small businesses. It helps owners improve practical business capability across time, follow-up, retained money, visibility, payment systems, operations, organization, technology, and operating support.

Start with recognizable business pain and plain language. Do not lead with technical consulting vocabulary when the owner/customer would understand the problem more naturally another way.

Business Freedom is not a separate 3P customer engagement. Professional expertise donated by 3P may support the program, but the organizations, revenues, agreements, responsibilities, and service obligations remain distinct unless a separate 3P engagement is explicitly created.

## 3. Marketplace funnel relationship

Some Marketplace experiences may route qualified Marketplace vendors into Business Freedom services. That relationship must be described accurately.

**Get Paid Faster — Set Up Your Payment Links** is the specific example:

- it is offered only to Rebel Ranch Marketplace vendors;
- it stems from Business Freedom's existing merchant-services and automated-payment-processing capability;
- it is a Marketplace-to-Business-Freedom funnel/entry offer;
- it is not one of the six core general-public Business Freedom services;
- it must not replace or redefine the broader **Automated Payment Processing** Business Freedom service; and
- an agent must preserve both sides of the relationship: Marketplace eligibility/placement and Business Freedom capability/fulfillment context.

Do not move or describe this offer in a way that erases either relationship.

## 4. What controls what

- `business-freedom-operations.md` — program authority: purpose, audience, service map, organization boundaries, claims limits, priorities, and documentation structure.
- `README.md` — current navigation/index and active work order.
- `brand.md` — approved Business Freedom visual and experience direction.
- `pages/*.md` — service/page-specific decisions, approvals, pricing direction, copy, scope, and release history.
- Root `AGENTS.md` — repository-wide work rules, authorization, verification, communication, recoverability, and completion standards.

A page record does not override the program authority. The program authority does not silently rewrite an owner-approved page-specific decision. Conflicts must be surfaced and resolved explicitly.

## 5. Agent expectations

Every Business Freedom agent must:

- preserve approved service names, scope, pricing presentation, claims boundaries, and customer-facing positioning;
- distinguish live, approved, planned, proposed, and unverified states;
- verify current code/public behavior before calling a page or feature live;
- keep Business Freedom separate from 3P except where an explicitly documented relationship applies;
- preserve Marketplace-specific eligibility when a Marketplace funnel is involved;
- use the Business Freedom brand rather than defaulting to generic RRM styling;
- avoid creating duplicate handoffs, duplicate authorities, or session-summary files when the information belongs in an existing current authority;
- use Git history for recovery instead of creating routine in-repo backup copies; and
- communicate with the owner in plain language.

## 6. Scope and owner approval

Do not add a new Business Freedom service, recurring offer, price, guarantee, public claim, release state, or cross-program funnel relationship without owner approval.

Do not treat an existing draft, filename, branch, design, or page record as authorization to publish or change a live surface.

When the owner has another agent actively building a specific landing page, do not rewrite that agent's page record or implementation merely because you are working on Business Freedom documentation. Stay inside the assigned scope.

## 7. Completion standard

A documentation update is complete only when:

- the correct current authority was updated;
- agent routing still points to valid files;
- no competing duplicate authority was created;
- the new wording does not contradict the current program map or owner decision; and
- the final repository diff contains only the authorized scope.
