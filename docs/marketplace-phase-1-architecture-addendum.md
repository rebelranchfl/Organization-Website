# Marketplace Phase 1 Architecture Addendum

Status: Approved architecture requirements for Gate 1 and future phases.

1. Future seller-team access must use explicit seller membership records and scoped roles. Public creator or household affiliations do not grant account access.
2. Public seller presentation must remain separate from private legal, tax, payout, licensing, insurance, and compliance details.
3. Compliance must be category-driven through requirements, assignments, attestations, credentials, verification, and expiration tracking.
4. The initial future commerce release must allow one seller per cart and one seller per order.
5. Integrated checkout requires a separate owner approval gate covering legal structure, taxes, payment provider, merchant-of-record responsibilities, refunds, disputes, and seller payouts.
6. Records require defined retention, soft deletion, archiving, suspension, appeal, reinstatement, account closure, and legal-preservation rules.
7. Operational notifications must cover submissions, changes requested, approvals, credential expiration, inquiries, orders, fulfillment, suspension, and appeals.
8. Public and seller experiences must support rural connectivity through compressed media, low JavaScript overhead, draft recovery, clear save states, mobile camera uploads, and directory access that does not depend on loading a map.
9. Map implementation requires owner approval of provider cost, geocoding privacy, coordinate retention, home-business approximation, and youth-location protections.
10. Marketplace access is independent of Creation Station paid membership and may receive only the narrow shared-data access approved for Marketplace.
