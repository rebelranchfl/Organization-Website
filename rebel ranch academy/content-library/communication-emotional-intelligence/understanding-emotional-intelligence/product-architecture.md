## Value proposition (what a learner leaves with)
Not just "emotional intelligence is real/not real" — a specific, evidence-backed thing to try (reappraisal) the next time a strong feeling shows up, plus enough of the real disagreement to not be fooled by an overconfident "EQ beats IQ" claim elsewhere.

## What this product is deliberately NOT
- Not a validated EI assessment instrument (no such instrument was researched or licensed). The added "Quick Quiz" is a plain comprehension check on this lesson's own content (the four abilities, IQ vs. EQ, reappraisal vs. suppression) — it does not measure the learner's emotional intelligence and does not claim to.
- Not a multi-session course.
- Not therapeutic or clinical guidance.
- Not personalized (no branching logic; the same page serves every reader, consistent with its Free/introductory role).

- The "is EI valid" debate is presented as unresolved, not resolved in RRA's favor or against it.
