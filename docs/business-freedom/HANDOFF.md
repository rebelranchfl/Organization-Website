## Automated Payment Processing boundary

This is the broad payment-processing service. It designs, sets up, creates, connects, automates, and streamlines merchant services, payment systems, and the software that supports them. Modern money movement is already highly automated after an owner approves a payment, transfer, bill, service, or subscription. The professional value is designing and automating the linkages between disconnected systems so the handoffs work with minimal repeated user input.

This service is distinct from:

- `Keep Your Money`, which follows every dollar across the whole operation, identifies root causes in connected systems and processes, and builds one clean flow so the financial result better reflects the work and sales.
- `Get Paid Faster — Set Up Your Payment Links`, the narrow $49.99 Marketplace offer for a seller who needs a basic way to accept online payment.
