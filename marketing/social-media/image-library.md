# Rebel Ranch Ministries Image Library

## Purpose

This file tracks approved images, Canva links, logo files, and design notes for Rebel Ranch social media.

The marketing agent should only use images that are listed here or clearly approved by Brooke.

The agent maintains this library, locates approved repository or Canva assets through available access, records links and usage, and checks reuse timing. Brooke only approves, rejects, corrects, or chooses between options when human judgment is needed; Brooke does not manually copy asset details or maintain a duplicate image tracker.

Before an approved post is scheduled or posted, the agent checks this file and `posted-archive.md`. Scheduling or posting still requires separate authorization.

Do not recreate the Rebel Ranch logo.
Use the real logo from the repo or approved Canva files.

---

## Image Rules

- Images must match Rebel Ranch Ministries style, tone, and colors.
- Locked rule: every image must match the specific program, message, and content bucket — not the word `Ranch`.
- Rebel Ranch Ministries is a responsible rebellion, a community resource hub, and a people-building movement. It is not a ranch-only or agriculture-only identity.
- Do not mix Rebel Ranch and 3P branding.
- Do not use fake ranch marks or recreated logos.
- Do not default to animals, fencing, feed, tools, boots, barns, dirt, hay, livestock, farm labor, cowboy styling, western fonts, rural scenery, or generic country graphics because the brand name contains `Ranch`.
- Farm and animal imagery is allowed only when it matches farm work, sustainability, agriculture, food, water, land, animal care, or a farm-specific lesson.
- Use real people, families, local businesses, creators, practical learning, community support, and hands-on work when they match the message.
- Donation posts should feel real and practical, not sad nonprofit guilt marketing.
- Business Freedom graphics should point back to Rebel Ranch Ministries, not 3P.
- The agent records Canva designs here with links so they can be reused and edited.

## Program Visual Match

- **Business Freedom:** owner relief, working sessions, laptops, calendars, calls, customer follow-up, money clarity, practical systems, visibility, and one-person business survival.
- **Rebel Ranch Marketplace:** real local sellers, makers, growers, food trucks, services, storefronts, products, community buying, and small-business visibility.
- **Creation Station:** creative supplies, hands-on projects, planning, making, confidence, follow-through, reflection, and finished work; family-friendly, not childish.
- **Rebel Ranch Academy:** match the actual lesson — communication, emotional intelligence, responsibility, accountability, confidence, courage, independence, family, teamwork, hard conversations, money, taxes, business, law, difficult people, difficult seasons, finding your tribe, or practical life skills. Use agriculture only for an agriculture-specific lesson.
- **Support the Mission:** show the whole people-building ecosystem through families, laptops, notebooks, planning materials, business support, vendor resources, creative supplies, Academy life-skill materials, community help, food/sustainability, and practical program funding. Never imply that support only buys ranch supplies or feeds animals.
- **Support the Mission test:** the visual message must be `Support the people-building work`, not `Help us buy ranch stuff`.
- **Farm / sustainability:** animals, fencing, feed, tools, gardens, food, water, land, and boots-on-the-ground work are appropriate when that is the actual subject.
- **Whole-ministry / general brand:** modern community hub, responsible ownership, practical independence, real skills, local people, family strength, small-business survival, creative growth, life readiness, and community rebuilding.

Before approving or using an image, the agent must be able to answer yes to all three:

1. Does the image match the actual program or message?
2. Would the image still make sense if the word `Ranch` were not in the organization name?
3. Does the image represent the full subject without shrinking Rebel Ranch Ministries into an animal, farm, western, or agriculture-only brand?
