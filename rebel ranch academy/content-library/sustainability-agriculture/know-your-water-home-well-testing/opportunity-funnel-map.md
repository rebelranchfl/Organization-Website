# RRA-2026-0002 — Opportunity Funnel Map

**Project:** Know Your Water — Home & Well Testing  
**AI-Agent:** ChatGPT/GPT-5.6 Sol  
**Session:** RRA Product Design Agent  
**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Mission entry

### Free working candidate — `What Should I Test — and Why?`

Purpose: help a private-well household stop guessing and leave with a useful first testing plan.

Required learner work:
- identify source;
- select intended use(s);
- identify last test/baseline status;
- identify recent event/change;
- identify local-risk clues;
- identify household vulnerability;
- distinguish routine vs event/local-risk questions;
- choose a practical next evidence route.

Required output:
- a concise Testing Question Map;
- local/certified resource next steps;
- what can be learned/prepared before results;
- what decisions should wait for adequate evidence.

The free layer must be genuinely useful and must not be a teaser that withholds basic safety education.

## Paid-depth working candidate — `My Well Test Plan + Result-to-Next-Action System`

Paid value must come from work performed for the learner, including:
- persistent household/well profile;
- multi-condition testing branches;
- routine/event/vulnerability/treatment-verification planning;
- test-route comparison;
- sample-preparation organizer tied to provider instructions;
- result-context record;
- confirmation/retest logic;
- treatment-job/source-correction handoff;
- recurring testing calendar;
- before/after verification;
- printable/offline household water-testing record.

No public price is authorized at Product Opportunity Research.

## Natural bundle paths

### Water Through the Layers
Relationship: the testing product identifies the treatment job; the parent Water product teaches broader treatment/system logic.

Boundary: do not duplicate treatment architecture here.

### Reading Water Test Results
Relationship: deeper units, limits, benchmarks and interpretation skill.

Boundary: candidate follow-on unless Product Design determines a minimal interpretation layer is necessary for V1.

### Post-Flood / Post-Repair Testing
Relationship: event-triggered branch with stronger operational workflow.

### Annual Well Care
Relationship: testing calendar + maintenance history + source/well inspection.

### Local Risk / Specialized Contaminants
Relationship: targeted PFAS/arsenic/radionuclide/pesticide/VOC branches only after location-specific evidence is sufficient.

## Opportunity Intelligence candidates

1. **What Should I Test — and Why?** — mission/free diagnostic entry.
2. **My Well Test Plan** — persistent testing-plan core.
3. **Reading Water Test Results** — result literacy and decision context.
4. **Treatment Verification Planner** — before/after evidence loop.
5. **Post-Flood / Post-Repair Well Testing** — event response.
6. **New Home / New Well Baseline Planner** — unknown-history intake.
7. **Affordable Testing Route Finder** — state/local/certified lab route navigator, only where data can be kept current.
8. **Local Risk Mapper** — structured research tool, not a false-certainty contaminant predictor.
9. **Sample Collection Failure Modes** — why samples get rejected/compromised and how to follow provider instructions.
10. **Annual Well Test + Maintenance Calendar** — recurring system support.

These are opportunity candidates, not authorization to add them to V1.

## Conversion logic

The ethical conversion path is:

`FREE UNDERSTANDING + FIRST PLAN → LEARNER SEES THE REAL COMPLEXITY OF THEIR OWN SYSTEM → PAID TOOL PERFORMS REPEATABLE ORGANIZATION / COMPARISON / RECORDKEEPING / DECISION WORK → BUNDLE ONLY WHEN A NEW JOB ACTUALLY EXISTS`

Do not manufacture anxiety to create conversion. The learner should be able to use the free layer even if they never buy anything.

## Product-value boundary

The paid layer fails if it becomes:
- more contaminant cards;
- a static annual checklist;
- a list of labs/vendors;
- generic result explanations;
- affiliate links;
- a PDF version of free EPA/Florida guidance.

The paid layer passes only if learner inputs materially change the plan, branch logic, route comparison, result record, schedule and next actions.

## TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE

The funnel itself should demonstrate the Academy principle:

**Give people enough knowledge to make a better decision for free; charge only when RRA performs meaningful additional work that saves research, comparison, organization or decision effort.**
