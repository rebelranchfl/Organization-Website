# Rebel Ranch Ministries Local Landscape

## Purpose

This file is the agent's running research record of local businesses,
chambers of commerce, community organizations, and events/happenings in
Rebel Ranch's service area. It is the research base `outreach-queue.md`
draws from.

This file does not duplicate `marketing/social-media/group-rules.md`
(the Facebook group audit) — see that file for community Facebook groups
and their posting rules. This file covers chambers, real-world
organizations, businesses, and events, not Facebook groups.

The agent maintains this file directly: adds new entries as it researches,
updates status, and records sources/dates. Brooke does not need to
research or maintain this file herself.

## Events / Happenings

### Newberry Watermelon Festival

- 81st annual festival: Saturday, May 16, 2026, 9:00am-4:00pm
- Location: CountryWay Town Square, 1739 SW 248th Dr, Newberry, FL 32669
- Vendor contact: nwfvendor@gmail.com
- Website: https://newberrywatermelonfestival.com/
- What it includes: free admission, food trucks, craft booths, live
  entertainment, and a parade that businesses, schools, and clubs are
  explicitly invited to enter (new for 2026: a golf cart competition with
  a watermelon theme).
- Fit: strong Marketplace/community-visibility opportunity — craft/vendor
  booth space and parade entry are both open to local organizations.
  Running since 1946; this is the single highest-visibility local event
  found so far.
- Outreach status: Not yet contacted — time-sensitive, vendor applications
  for a May event typically need action well before spring. See
  `outreach-queue.md`.
- Researched: 2026-08-16

### Alachua County Farmers Market (ACFM)

- Every Saturday, year-round, 8:30am-12pm, until sold out
- New location as of January 2026: San Felasco Tech City, Alachua, FL
- The only Growers-Only market in north-central Florida — everything
  sold is grown within 50 miles by the farmer who sells it.
- Website: https://www.441market.com/
- Fit: strong tie to Animal Husbandry/Rebel Ranch Academy sustainability
  work if Rebel Ranch ever sells or exhibits produce; also a place to
  meet other small growers who could be Marketplace-aligned.
- Related funding note: Alachua County reallocated $100,000 in Small
  Farmer Grants for farmers markets/small ag businesses for FY2026-2027 —
  logged in `marketing/funding/grant-research.md` as a new lead.
- Outreach status: Not yet contacted — lower priority than the vendor
  events above since it's a recurring weekly commitment (transportation
  consideration), not a one-time application. See `outreach-queue.md`.
- Researched: 2026-08-16
