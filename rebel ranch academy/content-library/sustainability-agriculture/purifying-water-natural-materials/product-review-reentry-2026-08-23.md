### Evidence-first education
**PASS.** `product-architecture.md` and the Responsible Rebellion manuscript addendum teach supported methods, functions, systems and comparisons before learner-specific testing is known. Uncertainty calibrates exact recommendations; it does not suppress general education.

### Multi-use / multi-branch
**PASS.** Intended uses are explicitly multi-select. Drinking/cooking, animals, irrigation/garden, emergency backup and other supported uses may coexist. The architecture requires shared infrastructure, branch points, branch-specific jobs and a readable whole-system view.

### Tangible systems and build concepts
**PASS.** The Product Design requires, at minimum, a 55-gallon layered filtration cutaway, 5-gallon gravity system, settling/prefiltration/storage sequence, source-to-use treatment train, well/pump/storage branching, treatment-function comparisons and scale translation. Each must teach what parts do, substitutions, strengths, limits and verification needs.

### Historical / regional / modern comparison
**PASS.** The design preserves approved historical/global material and requires comparison by function, context, local resources, infrastructure, strengths and limitations rather than labeling one era or institution as automatically correct.

### Use-what-you-have substitutions
**PASS.** `implementation/my-water-plan-spec.md` requires suitable existing resources, local/reuse options, worth-buying points and evidence-backed do-not-substitute boundaries.

### Testing as verification, not education permission
**PASS.** Testing is explicitly a decision/refinement tool. Before results, learners still receive source/use structure, general treatment functions, system families, storage/movement/control concepts, substitutions and an explanation of what the missing test would change. Home versus targeted/lab limits and affordable/free/local routes remain part of the approved design where verified.

### Living My Water Plan
**PASS.** The required output is branch-aware and materially personalized: source, all selected uses, branch diagram, shared infrastructure, knowns/unknowns, test decisions, system/build choices, functions, substitutions, buy points, verification, maintenance/retest, backup/failure plan and next three actions. Generic paragraph output fails the spec.

### Responsible experiment
**PASS.** The product requires `UNDERSTAND → BUILD / IMPLEMENT → OBSERVE → TEST → COMPARE → IMPROVE → DECIDE → TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE` and preserves the permanent rule: **A successful build is not proof. Test the result.**
