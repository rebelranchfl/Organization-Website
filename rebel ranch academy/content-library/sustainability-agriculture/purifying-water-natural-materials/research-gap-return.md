# RRA-2026-0001 — Product Design Evidence Gap Return

**Status:** TARGETED RESEARCH RETURN REQUIRED  
**Does this reopen Revision 3 generally?** No.  
**Scope:** UV microbiological water treatment/disinfection only.

## Why this return exists

Owner direction and Product Opportunity Research identified UV as an important treatment branch for private-well, household and other water scenarios. Current market/standards evidence confirms UV is a real residential treatment category, but UV is not developed deeply enough inside the owner-approved Revision 3 subject foundation for the Product Design Agent to write learner-facing factual instruction without crossing the approved evidence boundary.

This is therefore a **targeted additive evidence request**, not permission to replace or reopen the approved filtration/global/copper/frequency/lunar foundation.

## Research questions to answer

1. What water-treatment job does UV perform?
2. Which microorganisms can validated residential UV systems address, and under what conditions?
3. What is the distinction between NSF/ANSI 55 Class A and Class B systems?
4. What pretreatment/water-quality conditions affect UV effectiveness (for example turbidity or other relevant interference)?
5. What does UV **not** remove, especially dissolved chemicals/metals/salts and sediment?
6. Where should UV sit in a multi-stage treatment train?
7. What power, lamp replacement, sleeve cleaning, flow-rate and maintenance requirements matter?
8. What failure modes or false-security risks should learners understand?
9. How should UV differ between point-of-use and point-of-entry scenarios?
10. What claims can be made for private-well use, and what verification/testing should follow?
11. What is safe to explain/build educationally versus what should use a validated/certified purchased device?
12. Are there meaningful low-resource alternatives for the same microbiological-disinfection **function**, and what tradeoffs do they create?
