## Composite recommendation

**Average score: 4.5 / 5**

### Recommendation

**PURSUE NOW — build as an interactive free + paid system, not a standalone ebook.**

Recommended product structure:

1. **Free:** `Can I Use This Container?` quick inspection / suitability path.
2. **Paid core:** `Build My Water Storage System` — multi-use container audit, capacity/weight/redundancy planner, container anatomy inspection, maintenance/failure-mode tools and a living personalized plan.
3. **Later / bundle:** species-specific livestock, agricultural produce-contact, rainwater/cistern, emergency planning and automatic watering branches after their evidence is sufficient.

## Why Rebel Ranch Academy?

The closest free guides usually answer **how much emergency water to store and what basic container rules to follow**. Calculators answer **how many gallons**. Sellers answer **what container to buy**.

RRA can answer the harder middle question:

> **What can the containers I already have responsibly do in my actual multi-use water system, what must I fix, what should stay separate, what do I genuinely need to buy, and how do I maintain the whole system afterward?**

That difference is supported by the approved research and creates direct learner work/output rather than merely different branding.

## What changes if the learner implements this?

The learner gains the ability to:

- classify storage by real job rather than label;
- reject obvious unsafe potable reuse cases;
- keep potable, livestock, irrigation and higher-consequence agricultural needs distinct;
- inventory useful existing resources before buying;
- calculate volume and approximate stored-water weight;
- see the tradeoff between one large container and several smaller ones;
- identify lid, opening, spigot, access, support, exposure and cleaning weaknesses;
- create a maintenance/inspection routine;
- preserve a usable plan that updates as containers and needs change.

## Paid-value test

A paid offer passes only if the final product actually performs meaningful work for the learner: classification, branching, calculation, comparison, planning and maintenance outputs.

If Visual Production or Product Design collapses the product into generalized prose/cards with a printable checklist, **the paid-value test fails and the price recommendation must be reduced/reconsidered.**

## Pricing direction for later Product Design

Do not establish a public price at this stage.

Current market evidence shows:
- broad emergency-preparedness planner example: about **$29**;
- 55-gallon barrels commonly around **$89–$200**;
- complete barrel system example around **$139**.

Those hardware prices do not directly set educational-product value. They do show that avoiding one unnecessary container purchase or choosing a more appropriate system can create meaningful decision value. Final pricing must be based on the completed interactive capability and current educational comparables, not on equipment retail price.
