Auxiliary & Partner Program

We Are Not Looking
for Everyone.

We are looking for builders. People already doing the work who want the backing to do it bigger.

This is not a subscription service. This is a mission-fit review, an application process, and an ongoing governance relationship. We provide church-administered program sponsorship, approved use of ministry infrastructure, expense oversight, recordkeeping support, and strategic partnership — in exchange for accountability, transparency, and alignment with our mission.

Why We Do This

Our board operates with zero salary. Every person building this is a volunteer. We did that on purpose — money should never contaminate a mission this important.

We know trust is hard to come by right now. Some things are still pure. Some people still mean what they say. Find your tribe. We might be it.
