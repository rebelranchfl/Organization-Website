## Required suitability questions

Before reusing/substituting an item, ask:
- Does it actually perform the required function?
- Is its material suitable for the intended water/use?
- Do prior contents create a contamination concern?
- Can it tolerate the pressure, temperature, sunlight, load, and environment involved?
- Can it be cleaned and maintained adequately?
- Does the function require validated performance, certification, or measurement accuracy?
- What happens when it fails?

## Cost comparison

For each path record:
- upfront cost;
- required tools/installation;
- recurring consumables;
- maintenance/service;
- expected useful life where supportable;
- power/fuel;
- labor/time;
- replacement-part dependency;
- what existing expense/system it replaces or supplements.

Do not claim savings without explicit assumptions and current evidence.

## Think Like a Rebel check

**Use what you have before deciding what you need to buy** does not mean “avoid buying.” It means purchase after the function, performance requirement, and available resources are understood.
