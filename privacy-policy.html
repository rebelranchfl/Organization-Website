Privacy Policy

How we handle business-service requests

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Information you provide

When you request a business service, we collect the information you submit, such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, and description of your business needs.

How we use it

We use this information to review your request, contact you about it, confirm the appropriate service, and send related invoice and intake information. If you choose text message as your preferred contact method and provide consent, we may text you about that request.

How requests are processed

Business-service requests are submitted through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. Formspree processes the information you submit to deliver the request to Rebel Ranch Ministries.

Your choices

You may choose email or phone contact instead of text messages. If you have agreed to receive text messages, reply STOP to opt out. You may also contact us at rebelranchfl@gmail.com with questions about your request or this policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or services change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
