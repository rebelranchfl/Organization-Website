# Content Outline — RRA-2026-0011: What Is Emotional Intelligence?

1. **Hook / real moment** — a recognizable scene of reacting badly to a feeling vs. handling it well.
2. **Core explanation** — the four abilities (Perceive, Use, Understand, Manage), plain language, from Salovey & Mayer via the four-branch model.
3. **IQ vs. EQ** — defines IQ on its own terms (Wechsler deviation IQ, mean 100/SD 15) before contrasting it with EQ; book-smart vs. people-smart with two concrete, contrasting examples. Added per owner review 2026-09-14 — the original outline contrasted EQ against IQ without ever defining IQ.
4. **Where this came from** — Thorndike (1920) → Gardner (1983) → Salovey & Mayer (1990), one paragraph, positioned as UNDERSTAND/EXPLORE.
5. **Myth check** — Goleman's popular "EQ beats IQ" version vs. the narrower, better-evidenced academic version; Locke's objection; the honest middle ground (Joseph & Newman meta-analysis). This is the COMPARE step.
6. **Diagram 1** — the four abilities, visual.
7. **The one technique that's actually been tested** — reappraisal vs. suppression (Gross, 1998), with Diagram 2 (comparison visual).
8. **Try It** — "Match & Check" activity: matching exercise (ability → definition) plus a scenario checkbox exercise (reappraisal vs. suppression), APPLY step. Redesigned per owner review 2026-09-14 — replaced free-text paragraph answers, which owner rejected as impractical for this audience.
9. **Take It Further** — RULER structure for families/classrooms wanting a repeatable framework.
10. **REBEL RANCH PRINCIPLE** — personal, family, communication, work, money/leadership connections, and where the comparison stops being valid. Renamed from "TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE" per owner decision 2026-09-14 (doctrine-wide rename, see ACADEMY-CONTENT-PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md Section 7).
11. **Quick Quiz** — short multiple-choice/checkbox check of the lesson's core claims (four abilities, IQ vs. EQ, reappraisal vs. suppression). Added per owner review 2026-09-14.
12. **What this is not** — one-line, proportional safety note: not therapy/diagnosis.
13. **Close / CREATE step** — learner names one real upcoming moment they'll apply reappraisal to.
