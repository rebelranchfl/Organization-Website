## Weighted recommendation

**Overall recommendation: 4.45 / 5 — BUILD, with format discipline.**

The opportunity is strong because the research supports a reusable decision system. The market is too saturated for a generic workbook to deserve premium positioning.

## Why Rebel Ranch Academy?

RRA should win on the job it performs for the learner:

> take a messy real-life claim or disagreement, split it into examinable parts, trace the evidence, identify echoes and assumptions, inspect social pressure on both sides, calibrate confidence, and produce a decision-ready record.

That is different from simply teaching a vocabulary list of bias terms or asking whether a headline is trustworthy.

## What changes if the learner implements this?

The learner gains a repeatable method to:

- distinguish claim type from truth/evidence strength;
- stop treating a mixed claim as one indivisible argument;
- trace repeated claims back to source;
- compare independent evidence instead of counting echoes;
- identify what would change their mind;
- notice belonging/norm pressure without assuming the crowd is wrong;
- choose confidence proportional to evidence;
- choose action proportional to uncertainty and cost of error;
- revisit a conclusion when evidence changes.

## Paid-value test

**What work did RRA actually do that the learner would otherwise have to do themselves?**

The paid product must:

1. decompose the learner's claim into layers;
2. organize source/evidence tracing;
3. make independent-vs-repeated evidence visible;
4. walk the learner through opposite/alternative tests;
5. separate evidence pressure from social pressure;
6. calculate/present a confidence state from learner choices without pretending to determine objective truth automatically;
7. produce a living Decision Record with next action and unresolved questions;
8. let the learner update the record when new evidence appears;
9. provide scenario practice and transfer across domains.

If Visual Production delivers only a reading sequence and static worksheets, **the paid-value case fails**.

## Pricing implication

A low-cost worksheet market clusters around roughly $7–$27 in observed examples, while a comprehensive multi-year digital-literacy curriculum can cost $149–$349+ for homeschool. RRA should not price against the premium curriculum simply because the subject overlaps.

Product Design recommendation: **$24 one-time** for the core interactive product, with the free entry tool separate. This remains a proposal only and is not authorized for public use.

The $24 price is defensible only if the tangible output and repeat-use functionality described above are actually delivered.
