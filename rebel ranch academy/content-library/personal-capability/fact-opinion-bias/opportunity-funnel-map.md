# RRA-2026-0003 — Opportunity / Learning Web

**AI-Agent:** ChatGPT/GPT-5.6 Sol  
**Session:** RRA Product Design Agent

## Entry problems

- “Is this a fact or just somebody's opinion?”
- “Everyone is saying the same thing — does that mean it is verified?”
- “How do I know whether I am being biased too?”
- “How do I teach my teen not to believe every viral claim?”
- “We are arguing about what happened versus what it means.”
- “I have to make a business/money decision and I am not sure whether the story matches the evidence.”

## Learning web

`Claim sorting` ↔ `Source tracing` ↔ `Evidence strength`  
`Evidence strength` ↔ `Bias mirror` ↔ `Social influence`  
`Social influence` ↔ `Confidence calibration` ↔ `Decision record`  
`Decision record` → `Family / Money / Business / Leadership / Media transfer`

## Prerequisite learning

No formal prerequisite. The free claim sorter teaches the minimum shared language.

## Follow-on learning

- difficult conversations;
- emotional regulation under disagreement;
- business problem diagnosis;
- financial decision-making;
- AI/media literacy;
- persuasive language and advertising;
- statistical reasoning;
- source/research literacy.

## Cross-Academy connections

- **Personal Strength & Independence:** independent judgment, accountability for one's own assumptions.
- **Communication & Emotional Intelligence:** separate event from interpretation; reduce motive-reading.
- **Business & Operations:** measure before solving the story; separate metric, hypothesis and recommendation.
- **Money, Finance & Taxes:** verify claims, compare evidence and cost of error before acting.
- **Family, Community & Leadership:** group pressure, dissent, norms and responsible leadership.

## Cross-program boundary

Business decision education may support Marketplace sellers or Business Freedom participants, but this product remains Rebel Ranch Academy education. It does not become Business Freedom consulting and does not merge program identities.
