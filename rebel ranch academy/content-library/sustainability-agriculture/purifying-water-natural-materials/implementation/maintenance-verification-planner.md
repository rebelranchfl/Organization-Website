## UV-specific maintenance rule

For any selected UV device, obtain the exact current manufacturer instructions for that certified model. Record:
- class/model/configuration;
- certified flow;
- required incoming-water conditions/pretreatment;
- lamp/LED service requirement;
- sleeve/optical-path cleaning requirement;
- alarm/sensor response;
- power-failure behavior;
- replacement-part requirements;
- post-treatment storage/distribution hygiene requirements.

Do not substitute a generic schedule for the exact device instructions.

## Retesting triggers

Record scenario-specific triggers such as:
- scheduled private-well testing baseline;
- treatment-system change;
- flooding/repair/contamination event;
- unusual change in water;
- failed component/alarm/power condition;
- suspected recontamination;
- other evidence-based trigger from the approved research.

## Transfer the principle
