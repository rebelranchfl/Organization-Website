Now Accepting Producers

Goal: Full Producer Network by End of 2026 — Applications Are Limited

Glyphosate in your oats. Atrazine in your water. PFAS in your blood. These are not conspiracy theories — they are documented chemicals found in commercial food, approved by agencies that receive funding from the industries they regulate. The result: a food system that has quietly poisoned an entire population while labeling it safe.

In Gilchrist County — the spring capital of the world, one of Florida's most naturally pristine regions — large commercial farming operations are contaminating the very waters that define this place. Local farmers who want to do things right cannot compete. USDA regulations, commercial licensing requirements, and corporate distribution systems make sure of that.

The Rebel Ranch Marketplace cuts all of that out. Real food from local people you can look in the eye. Private exchange within a member community where the relationship between producer and neighbor replaces every middleman. Accountability lives where it belongs — between real people who share the same land, the same water, and the same stake in getting this right.
