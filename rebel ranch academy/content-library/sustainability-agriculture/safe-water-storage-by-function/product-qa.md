## Paid-value test

**What work does RRA do that the learner would otherwise have to do?**

The designed product:
- separates multiple real storage jobs;
- inventories existing containers;
- forces prior-history inspection;
- classifies each container differently by branch where needed;
- calculates capacity and approximate water weight;
- compares redundancy/portability tradeoffs;
- exposes closure, dispensing, cleaning, placement and support failures;
- prioritizes corrections;
- creates a maintenance loop;
- generates a living personalized storage plan.

This is materially more than `more words to read`.

## Material evidence boundaries carried forward

Visual Production must not invent:
- species-specific livestock intake/quality thresholds;
- crop/commodity-specific produce-contact standards beyond approved general distinctions;
- universal material-migration claims for reused containers;
- universal shelf-life claims;
- universal `SAFE` classification.

7. Confirm print/offline output preserves the learner's populated plan.
