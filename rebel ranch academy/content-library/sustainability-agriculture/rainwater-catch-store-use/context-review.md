## RRA direction fit

The opportunity is especially strong for `Think Like a Rebel`: define the water job first, understand catchment/storage/distribution functions, use existing roof/elevation/resources where suitable, know where substitutions stop being equivalent, and verify performance instead of assuming a barrel of clear-looking water is safe for every use.
