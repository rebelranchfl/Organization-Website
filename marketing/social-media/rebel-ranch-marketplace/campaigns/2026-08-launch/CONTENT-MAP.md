## Existing visual categories

### Problem / recognition
**Message:** Facebook posts disappear, vendors are hard to find again, services are scattered, and big business gets most of the attention.  
**Purpose:** make viewers recognize their own frustration before introducing the solution.  
**Primary CTA:** comment / relate to the problem.

### Seller benefit
**Message:** free to join, no marketplace fees, easier to find, connect directly with buyers, fairer visibility for small local operators.  
**Purpose:** recruit supply before driving heavy buyer traffic.  
**Primary CTA:** tag or sign up a grower, maker, or tradesperson.

### Buyer benefit
**Message:** find local food, handmade goods, and local services without chasing scattered posts or guessing where to look.  
**Purpose:** explain the practical reason to return and use the platform.  
**Primary CTA:** search / browse / share with another local buyer.

### Community benefit
**Message:** keeps money local, supports small operators, builds real relationships, strengthens the community.  
**Purpose:** connect product utility to the larger Rebel Ranch Ministries mission.  
**Primary CTA:** share if local matters.

### Community input
**Message:** would you use this, what do you want to find locally, what is missing, what services are hardest to find?  
**Purpose:** comments, market research, community ownership, and feature/category discovery.  
**Primary CTA:** comment/message ideas.

### Rebel Ranch Ministries role
**Message:** Rebel Ranch Ministries built the system to reconnect local people with useful skills, products, services, and opportunities already present in the community.  
**Purpose:** trust and mission alignment without turning the reel into an institutional nonprofit ad.  
**Primary CTA:** follow the mission / help build it.

### Seller onboarding
**Message:** apply → build listing/storefront → show products/services → connect directly with buyers.  
**Purpose:** remove uncertainty and increase completed registrations.  
**Primary CTA:** sign up.

### Buyer onboarding
**Message:** browse/search → choose Marketplace, Goods, or Services → find seller/provider → connect directly.  
**Purpose:** drive actual platform use, not just awareness.  
**Primary CTA:** search local.

## Launch principles

1. **Seller supply first, buyer demand second, community engagement throughout.** Do not send large buyer traffic to an empty experience.
2. **Use real screenshots.** Show the actual directory, seller storefront, dashboard/listing experience, and how a buyer moves through the app.
3. **One reel = one job.** Do not ask viewers to like, share, follow, comment, sign up, and shop all at once.
4. **Rotate CTA types.** Comment, tag, share, follow, sign up, search, or tell us what is missing.
5. **Avoid repetitive visuals.** Mix problem graphics, real screenshots, people, product closeups, category spotlights, seller examples, buyer examples, and community scenes.
6. **Use Rebel Ranch Ministries when naming the organization.** `Rebel Ranch Local` is the product/program identity; `Rebel Ranch Ministries` is the organization behind it.
7. **Reels are assembled from individual vertical graphics.** Each graphic should work as a short visual beat in a Canva-built video, with text readable quickly on a phone.

## Conversion signals to watch

- **Awareness:** reel views, non-follower reach, profile visits.
- **Engagement:** comments, shares, tags, saves.
- **Seller acquisition:** application starts, completed seller signups, listings created.
- **Buyer activation:** directory/search visits, listing views, messages/contact actions.
- **Community intelligence:** repeated requests for specific foods, goods, services, towns, or features.

Use performance data to adjust the second week rather than continuing a fixed sequence if one message clearly outperforms another.
