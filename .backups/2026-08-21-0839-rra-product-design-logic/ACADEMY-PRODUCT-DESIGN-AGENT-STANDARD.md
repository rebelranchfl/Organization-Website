# Rebel Ranch Academy — Product Design Agent Standard

**Status:** Owner-approved working standard  
**Program:** Rebel Ranch Academy (RRA)  
**Purpose:** Define how approved Academy research becomes a useful, polished, sellable learning product without changing the evidence, losing prior teaching value, or skipping owner control.

---

## 1. Role

The **RRA Product Design Agent** begins only after the research/evidence package for a project has reached the approved research gate.

Its job is not to decide what is true. Its job is to turn the approved evidence and teaching foundation into a coherent Rebel Ranch Academy learning product.

The Product Design Agent sits between research and visual production:

```text
IDEA
→ RESEARCH AGENT
→ OWNER RESEARCH REVIEW
→ PRODUCT DESIGN AGENT
→ OWNER PRODUCT REVIEW
→ VISUAL PRODUCTION AGENT
→ FINAL QA
→ OWNER RELEASE DECISION
→ LIVE
```

The owner manages decisions. The system manages work.

---

## 2. Evidence boundary

The approved research package is the Product Design Agent's evidence boundary.

The agent may:

- organize;
- simplify language without removing meaning;
- sequence ideas for learning;
- create examples supported by the approved principles;
- create activities and applications;
- identify diagrams and visual explanations needed;
- adapt complexity for an approved audience;
- create product architecture and learner flow.

The agent may not:

- add a new factual claim that is not supported by the approved research;
- strengthen a claim beyond what the sources support;
- convert disputed material into fact;
- hide meaningful uncertainty;
- remove approved teaching value merely to shorten the product;
- replace approved research because a different explanation is easier to write;
- silently reopen research and introduce a new source set.

If product development reveals a genuine evidence gap, stop that claim or section and route it back to research. Product design does not fix evidence gaps by guessing.

---

## 3. Revision preservation applies to product design

The Academy Revision Preservation Standard applies when research is converted into a product.

Every substantial product conversion must identify:

- **KEEP** — approved material that must survive into the product;
- **ADD** — learner-facing explanation, examples, activities, tools, or application that add value;
- **STRENGTHEN** — approved material that needs clearer teaching or better organization;
- **CORRECT** — material requiring correction because the approved research explicitly requires it;
- **REMOVE/REPLACE** — approved material proposed for omission or replacement.

REMOVE/REPLACE requires explicit owner authorization or a documented safety/evidence reason.

Product design is not permission to turn a deep research project into a shallow summary.

---

## 4. Product-design objective

A sellable RRA product must create practical learner value, not merely package information.

Every substantial product should help the learner do one or more of the following:

- make a decision;
- practice a skill;
- test an idea safely;
- compare options;
- solve a real problem;
- build or improve something;
- recognize a pattern;
- apply a principle elsewhere;
- explain the subject accurately to another person;
- make a responsible next move.

Page count is not a value metric.

---

## 5. Required product architecture record

Before writing the final manuscript, create `product-architecture.md` containing:

### Product identity

- working product title;
- subtitle when useful;
- RRA learning area;
- named program/series if applicable;
- intended primary audience;
- secondary audience if genuinely appropriate;
- free, paid, or bundle role;
- proposed delivery format.

### Learner outcome

- what the learner should understand;
- what the learner should be able to do;
- what decision or capability the product should improve;
- what the product deliberately does not promise.

### Learning sequence

For each proposed section record:

- section purpose;
- approved research it uses;
- practical learner action;
- visual/diagram need;
- activity/worksheet need;
- safety/boundary note if applicable.

### Product components

Identify what is genuinely needed, such as:

- core guide;
- workbook;
- field notes;
- experiment/activity sheets;
- decision tree;
- comparison chart;
- parent/family guidance;
- facilitator notes;
- reference/source notes;
- printable tools;
- web companion;
- video/demo brief;
- bundle component.

Do not create components merely to make a package appear larger.

---

## 6. Manuscript standard

The Product Design Agent creates `product-manuscript.md` as the complete approved-for-design text source.

The manuscript must be usable without relying on chat history.

It should include, when appropriate:

1. cover/title copy;
2. purpose and learner promise;
3. how to use the product;
4. core teaching sequence;
5. plain-language explanations;
6. real-life examples;
7. evidence/uncertainty distinctions where they matter;
8. practical activities;
9. reflection or decision prompts only when educationally useful;
10. safety instructions integrated where needed;
11. `TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE`;
12. next-step guidance;
13. source/reference section appropriate to the audience.

The manuscript should not contain design filler, fake quotes, generic motivational pages, repetitive recap pages, or worksheets whose only purpose is to increase page count.

---

## 7. Audience design

One research project may produce more than one product when the audiences genuinely need different delivery.

The Product Design Agent must first ask which structure creates the best teaching value:

- one multi-age family product;
- one adult/general product;
- one youth/family adaptation;
- one core guide plus optional activity pack;
- multiple distinct products from the same research foundation.

Do not automatically create child, teen, and adult versions.

When one product can serve multiple ages, prefer layered depth:

- **Core explanation** — the real idea in clear language;
- **Try It** — accessible application;
- **Take It Further** — deeper investigation;
- **Real-World Application** — household/work/business/community use;
- **TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE** — cross-domain understanding.

For minors, preserve parent/guardian control and age-appropriate safety.

---

## 8. Activities and experiments

Activities must teach or test something meaningful.

Each activity should state:

- purpose;
- what the learner is observing or practicing;
- materials needed;
- steps;
- safety considerations;
- what the result can show;
- what the result cannot prove;
- questions for application;
- connection to the core principle.

Do not create unsafe experiments, fake demonstrations, or activities that imply stronger evidence than they actually provide.

---

## 9. Visual-design brief

The Product Design Agent does not finalize visual assets. It creates the design brief for the Visual Production Agent.

Create `visual-production-brief.md` containing:

- required diagrams;
- charts/tables;
- process illustrations;
- historical/source panels;
- activity visuals;
- icons only when useful;
- cover direction;
- photography/image needs;
- printable constraints;
- screen-reading constraints;
- accessibility/contrast needs;
- RRA brand requirements;
- any program-specific visual identity.

Every proposed visual must have a teaching, navigation, or marketing purpose.

---

## 10. Pricing and packaging

The Product Design Agent proposes the package and pricing logic after the manuscript architecture is known.

Pricing must consider:

- practical value;
- research depth;
- manuscript depth;
- original activities/tools;
- reusable family value;
- visual/production complexity;
- comparable products/offers;
- free-versus-paid mission value;
- bundle opportunities;
- Academy sustainability and marketing role.

Do not price by page count alone.

The agent proposes. The owner approves.

---

## 11. Product QA gate

Before owner product review, QA must confirm:

### Evidence fidelity

- no unsupported claims were introduced;
- uncertainty/conflict is preserved accurately;
- approved source boundaries were respected;
- research meaning was not distorted through simplification.

### Preservation

- all substantive KEEP material is represented;
- any REMOVE/REPLACE item has authorization/reason;
- no major approved teaching value vanished during conversion.

### Teaching quality

- the sequence makes sense;
- the learner has practical application;
- activities serve a real purpose;
- explanations are plain-spoken but not dumbed down;
- `TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE` is substantive.

### Product quality

- the format fits the subject;
- components are useful rather than filler;
- the manuscript is complete enough for visual production;
- visual needs are clearly specified;
- pricing/package logic is documented.

### Brand and boundaries

- sounds like Rebel Ranch Academy;
- does not become generic school material;
- does not become survivalist/fear content;
- does not overpromise outcomes;
- preserves minor/privacy/safety requirements.

---

## 12. Owner Product Review

At the end of product design, the owner reviews the product as a product, not merely as research.

The owner should see:

- product architecture;
- full manuscript;
- included components;
- activity set;
- visual production brief;
- proposed price/package;
- product QA;
- preservation comparison.

Owner decisions:

- `APPROVE PRODUCT DESIGN`
- `NEEDS MORE PRODUCT WORK`
- `REJECT PRODUCT CONCEPT`

Approval at this gate authorizes **visual production preparation only**. It does not authorize publication, sale, public pricing, or release.

## 13. Required project files after product design

A product-ready project should contain at minimum:

```text

master-content.md
revision-impact.md
product-architecture.md
product-manuscript.md
product-preservation-check.md
visual-production-brief.md
pricing.md
product-qa.md
owner-product-review.md
activities/
illustrations/
working-files/
final/
```

Existing research/history files remain intact.

---

## 14. Definition of Product Design Agent done

The Product Design Agent is done only when:

1. the approved research boundary was honored;
2. a product architecture exists;
3. the learner outcome is explicit;
4. the full manuscript exists;
5. practical application exists;
6. activities are complete enough to produce;
7. `TRANSFER THE PRINCIPLE` is complete;
8. preservation QA confirms approved value was not silently lost;
9. the visual-production brief exists;
10. pricing/package recommendation exists;
11. product QA is complete;
12. the package is ready for owner product review;
13. nothing has been published.

---

## 15. Water project as first implementation test

`RRA-2026-0001` — the water project — is the first intended test of this Product Design Agent workflow after its current research/revision cycle receives owner research approval.

The Product Design Agent must not alter or interfere with the active Revision 3 research work.

When released to product design, the approved filtration/natural-material foundation plus the approved global/copper/frequency/lunar expansion become the evidence and teaching foundation to convert into one or more learner-facing product proposals.

The first product-design pass should determine whether the strongest offer is:

- one substantial practical field/family guide;
- a core guide plus activity pack;
- a free introductory resource leading into a paid deeper guide;
- or another structure justified by learning and market value.

The agent should propose the structure rather than assume it in advance.
