## UV decision branch

Use only when **microbiological disinfection/inactivation** is an identified treatment job.

Before selecting a potable-water UV system, confirm:
- the water problem has been diagnosed/tested sufficiently for the decision;
- required pretreatment/clarity conditions are addressed;
- correct NSF/ANSI 55 class is understood;
- exact model/configuration and certified flow match the use;
- POU versus POE scope matches the delivery problem;
- other chemical/sediment/dissolved-contaminant jobs are handled separately where needed;
- power and downstream recontamination risks are considered;
- model-specific maintenance can actually be performed.

### Potable-water boundary

**WORTH BUYING / DO NOT IMPROVISE THE SAFETY-CRITICAL REACTOR:** use a validated/certified UV reactor appropriate to the identified job rather than treating a homemade/generic UV light arrangement as equivalent.

## Compare disinfection forms by function

When relevant compare validated UV, boiling, chlorine-based disinfection, and solar disinfection by:
- required resources;
- volume/throughput;
- operating conditions;
- time;
- power/fuel/consumables;
- residual protection;
- maintenance/operator discipline;
- limitations;
- verification.

Do not call them universal substitutes simply because they can all contribute to microbial-control functions under some conditions.
