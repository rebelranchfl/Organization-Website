One Creation Station identity · Age-aware support

Built for creators at different stages.

Studio membership is not limited to one age group. The language, controls, and amount of adult involvement change with the creator; the core path stays connected.

Ages 6–12

Young Creators

Children receive guided structure while a parent or guardian holds the account, sees relevant progress, and controls anything that could become public.

Ages 13–17

Teen Creators

Teens can build independence, presentation skills, and practical business awareness while public decisions remain under parent or guardian control.

Ages 18+

Adult Makers

Adults may bring existing work and move more directly toward a personal Studio page without completing the full child-guided project journey.

The Studio membership path

From creative idea to a page that feels like theirs.

Young and Teen Creators may use the full guided journey. Adult Makers can begin with existing work. In both cases, the public Studio information is submitted separately and reviewed before publication.
