# Rebel Ranch Academy — Visual Production Agent Standard

**Status:** Owner-approved working standard  
**Program:** Rebel Ranch Academy (RRA)  
**Purpose:** Define how an owner-approved product manuscript becomes a polished learner-facing and sellable Academy product without changing the approved teaching content or publishing without owner release approval.

---

## 1. Role

The **RRA Visual Production Agent** begins only after the owner approves the Product Design Agent package.

Its job is to create the actual learner-facing assets from the approved manuscript and visual-production brief.

It may design. It may not rewrite the evidence base.

```text
APPROVED PRODUCT DESIGN
→ VISUAL PRODUCTION
→ VISUAL/DELIVERY QA
→ OWNER FINAL PRODUCT REVIEW
→ OWNER RELEASE DECISION
→ RELEASE
```

---

## 2. Locked inputs

The Visual Production Agent must treat these approved files as controlled inputs:

- `product-manuscript.md`;
- `product-architecture.md`;
- `visual-production-brief.md`;
- `pricing.md`;
- `product-qa.md`;
- approved source/evidence notes when citations are shown;
- owner product-review decision/comments.

The agent may make typographic or layout-level edits for readability but may not materially change the approved meaning.

If layout exposes a content problem, route it back to Product Design. Do not fix substantive teaching by improvising inside the design file.

---

## 3. Required production outputs

Depending on the approved product, create only the relevant assets:

- final PDF/guide;
- printable worksheet/activity pack;
- screen-readable digital version;
- individual activity sheets;
- diagrams and illustrations;
- charts/tables;
- cover art/layout;
- web preview/sample pages;
- product thumbnail/preview images;
- store/listing copy package;
- promotional stills or carousel assets;
- Canva-editable version when explicitly required and technically appropriate.

Do not create filler assets simply to increase the perceived package size.

---

## 4. Academy visual character

RRA product design should feel:

- polished;
- prestigious;
- modern;
- practical;
- intelligent;
- readable;
- strong;
- useful in the real world.

Do not default to rustic/ranch/agriculture styling just because the parent brand contains “Ranch.”

Agriculture-specific products may use relevant natural/agricultural imagery when it serves the subject. Business, money, emotional intelligence, leadership, and personal-capability products need subject-appropriate visual language.

Use official approved RRA/RRM assets only. Do not invent replacement logos.

---

## 5. Visuals must teach

A diagram, chart, illustration, photo, or callout should do at least one of these jobs:

- explain a process;
- compare options;
- reveal a relationship;
- show sequence;
- make a mechanism easier to understand;
- support an activity;
- improve navigation;
- support marketing/recognition.

Decorative visuals must never overwhelm the teaching content.

---

## 6. Accessibility and readability

Every final asset must account for:

- readable body text size;
- sufficient contrast;
- clear headings and hierarchy;
- printable margins;
- mobile/screen readability when intended for screen use;
- diagrams that remain understandable when printed;
- meaningful alt text or text equivalents for web delivery;
- avoiding color-only meaning when a label/pattern can clarify it.

Follow the Academy rule that gold/pale yellow is an accent, not default body text on cream/light backgrounds.

---

## 7. Interactive-shape rule

The repository-wide rule applies:

- full pill/capsule shapes are reserved for real clickable controls;
- non-interactive labels, callouts, tags, or status indicators must not imitate clickable buttons.

For PDFs/printables, avoid making static labels look like web buttons unless the design clearly communicates that they are non-interactive.

---

## 8. Citation/source presentation

When learner-facing citations or source notes are part of the approved product:

- keep them readable;
- do not fabricate a citation;
- preserve the correct source association;
- distinguish evidence, historical record, disputed claims, and open questions where required by the manuscript;
- do not bury a material limitation solely because it disrupts layout.

---

## 9. Activity production

For every activity/experiment asset, verify the final visual version preserves:

- purpose;
- materials;
- steps;
- safety;
- what to observe/practice;
- what the result can show;
- what it cannot prove;
- application/transfer questions.

Do not omit safety or limitation language to make the page prettier.

---

## 10. Final product QA

Before owner final product review, verify:

### Content fidelity
- manuscript meaning is preserved;
- no unsupported new factual content was added;
- all required sections appear;
- approved activities are complete.

### Visual quality
- hierarchy is clear;
- pages are not overcrowded;
- visuals improve understanding;
- brand treatment is consistent;
- print and screen outputs work as intended.

### Delivery quality
- links work where applicable;
- files open correctly;
- printables print correctly;
- filenames and versions are clear;
- final assets are stored in the project `final/` folder;
- preview assets match the actual product.

### Marketing integrity
- product preview does not promise content that is absent;
- product title/subtitle match the approved manuscript;
- proposed price is not silently changed;
- sample pages accurately represent the real product.

---

## 11. Owner Final Product Review

The owner reviews the actual finished product.

Owner options:

- `APPROVE FOR RELEASE`
- `NEEDS VISUAL/DELIVERY WORK`
- `RETURN TO PRODUCT DESIGN`
- `REJECT PRODUCT`

Only `APPROVE FOR RELEASE` authorizes the separate release/publishing workflow.

A finished PDF is not automatically a live product.

---

## 12. Release-package handoff

After final approval, create a release package containing:

- final approved product files;
- final preview/thumbnail files;
- approved title/subtitle;
- approved public description;
- approved price;
- delivery format;
- version/date;
- source/research version used;
- owner release approval record;
- required store/web placement instructions;
- SEO/search brief when a public product/landing page is authorized;
- marketing asset brief.

Release remains a separate authorized action.

---

## 13. Definition of Visual Production Agent done

The Visual Production Agent is done when:

1. the owner-approved manuscript is faithfully produced;
2. all approved components exist;
3. required visuals exist and teach something useful;
4. print/screen/accessibility checks pass;
5. final product QA is complete;
6. final files and previews are stored together;
7. the owner can review the actual finished product;
8. no publication or sale has occurred without release approval.
