The opportunity solves a real gap between **getting a water test** and **knowing what the report actually means**. The prior RRA-2026-0002 project researches how to decide what to test. This project begins after the report comes back and asks what can responsibly be concluded from the numbers, qualifiers, units and benchmarks.

## Why it belongs in Sustainability & Agriculture
Water-quality interpretation supports household water, private wells, homesteads, livestock/land systems and responsible treatment decisions. It is a practical resource-management skill rather than a laboratory-science course for its own sake.
