# RRA-2026-0004 — Pricing & Packaging Recommendation

**Status:** Product Design recommendation only. No public price, sale, release or storefront action is authorized.

## Recommended packaging

### Free mission entry
**Can I Use This Container? — Quick Check**

Standalone value:
- intended-use multi-select;
- prior-history red flags;
- closure/dispensing/cleanability check;
- basic branch-specific result;
- one clear next action.

Do not withhold safety-critical conclusions behind payment.

### Paid core
**Build My Water Storage System**

Includes:
- multi-use branch planning;
- repeatable Use What You Have container inventory;
- container-history gate;
- container anatomy inspection;
- capacity + weight + redundancy planning;
- branch assignments and corrections;
- maintenance planner;
- failure-mode activity;
- living My Water Storage Plan;
- printable/offline result;
- optional technical/evidence depth.

## Proposed price

**$29 one-time** for the complete interactive planning product.

### Why this level
- Current broad preparedness planner comparable: about $29 (DisasterPrepCalc, checked 2026-08-22).
- Free water guides/calculators are common, so price cannot be justified by information alone.
- Current purpose-made 55-gallon storage hardware commonly runs roughly $89–$200, with a complete barrel system example around $139; RRA should not price from hardware value, but the learner's container-choice decisions have meaningful practical stakes.
- $29 is justified only if the implemented product actually performs classification, multi-use branching, calculations, inventory analysis, maintenance planning and a personalized repeat-use output.

### Automatic price failure condition
If the release candidate becomes mainly generalized prose, cards, static checklists or a blank printable template, the $29 case fails and must be reconsidered before Product Review/Release.

## Bundle direction
Natural later bundle candidates:
- Water Through the Layers;
- Know Your Water — Home & Well Testing;
- rainwater/cistern planning;
- livestock watering systems.

Bundle pricing is not recommended until those products have approved independent value and current market evidence.
