## Free

### What Am I Actually Looking At?

Price: **Free**.

Reason: foundational claim-type/evidence-strength education is widely available free/cheap and is mission-useful. RRA's free version should demonstrate the stronger two-label method and provide real value without withholding a basic literacy concept.

## Paid core

### Think It Through

Proposed price: **$24 one-time**.

The recommendation sits above observed $6.99 single lessons and around observed $21–$27 short classroom units, while remaining far below comprehensive multi-year digital-literacy curricula priced $149–$349+.

This price is justified only if the finished product provides the tangible repeat-use outputs in `product-architecture.md`: claim decomposition, source/echo map, stress test, social-pressure map, confidence/action logic, living Decision Record and scenario practice.

If the final deliverable becomes mostly reading + static worksheets, lower the price or make it free; do not preserve $24 by adding more words.

## No unsupported value claims

Do not claim the product will save a universal dollar amount, eliminate bias, prevent misinformation, improve grades, or guarantee better decisions. The value proposition is capability, reduced research/organization burden, repeat-use structure and better evidence discipline.
