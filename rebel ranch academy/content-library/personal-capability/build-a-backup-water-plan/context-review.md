## Why this belongs in Personal Capability

This is broader than a water-treatment lesson. The core skill is continuity planning: identify the critical function, identify what can fail, establish reserve capacity, create alternate routes, prioritize limited resources, verify recovery, and maintain the plan before it is needed.

It naturally connects to Sustainability & Agriculture, especially private wells, cisterns, animals, irrigation and water storage, but the primary learning outcome is household capability and resilience.
