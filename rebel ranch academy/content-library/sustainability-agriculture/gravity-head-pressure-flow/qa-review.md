## Evidence QA
- 13 agent-vetted sources support the central physics, field hydraulics, gravity-system behavior and historical comparisons.
- The `2.31 ft ≈ 1 psi` / `0.433 psi per ft` relationship is corroborated independently.
- Static pressure, dynamic pressure, flow, velocity, storage capacity and friction are kept distinct.
- Ideal Bernoulli teaching is not used as a substitute for real pipe-loss analysis.
- Pipe diameter, length, roughness, fittings, flow and elevation are identified as performance variables supported by direct engineering/extension evidence.
- Gravity flow, siphon and pump functions are distinguished without claiming one is universally superior.
- Historical qanat, aflaj and Roman-aqueduct examples are used as evidence of gravity-system design, not as automatic proof of modern potability/suitability.
