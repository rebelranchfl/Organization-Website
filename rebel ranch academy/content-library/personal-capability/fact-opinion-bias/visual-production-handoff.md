## Required Visual Production behavior

Visual Production must preserve:

1. multiple simultaneous claim layers;
2. multiple sources and source-root/echo relationships;
3. multiple social-pressure pathways;
4. exact-place return from `Why?`, research, examples, sources, and other depth routes;
5. learner answers/state through ordinary deeper navigation;
6. dependent-output updates when earlier answers change where practical;
7. the living `My Decision Record` as a materially personalized result;
8. visual-first teaching before technical depth;
9. plain language first, with technical/evidence depth optional;
10. no claim that software automatically determines truth or diagnoses bias;
11. privacy-minimized learner signals only as specified in `learner-signal-plan.md`;
12. no dead links or 404 routes;
13. no clipped text;
14. no pill/capsule styling for non-clickable labels, tags, badges, statuses, or decorative text;
15. all approved evidence, uncertainty, preservation, minor/privacy, and safety boundaries.

## Required visual teaching

The authorized `visual-production-brief.md` requires the mixed-claim split visual, two-label claim/evidence map, source-root/echo map, Bias Mirror, social-pressure map, Unanimous Room comparison, confidence ladder, living My Decision Record, and Transfer web.

These visuals must teach or help the learner act; they are not decorative filler.
