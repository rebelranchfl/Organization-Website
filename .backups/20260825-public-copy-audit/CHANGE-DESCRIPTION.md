# Public copy audit — Programs, Business Freedom, Homepage

Date: 2026-08-25

Owner direction: public-facing page copy must speak to the visitor. Internal architecture, UX reasoning, agent explanations, and implementation language must not appear as customer-facing marketing copy.

Scope:
- Audit programs.html, business-fixes.html, and index.html.
- Correct internal/agent-facing language where found.
- Preserve existing interaction structure and functionality.
- Correct stale Business Freedom routes found during the copy pass.
- Keep Rebel Ranch Ministries voice direct, grounded, human, practical, community-centered, and free of corporate/AI-sounding explanation.

Primary findings:
- programs.html contained phrases such as “learn our organization,” “narrow the ecosystem,” “pathways,” “Academy-owned program,” “clear boundary,” and future implementation commentary that read like internal architecture notes rather than public marketing.
- business-fixes.html contained smaller UX-explanation phrases such as “instead of making you read a catalog first” and “the problem-first selector is the fastest route.”
- index.html was already substantially visitor-facing after its digital-experience rebuild; no broad rewrite required in this pass.
