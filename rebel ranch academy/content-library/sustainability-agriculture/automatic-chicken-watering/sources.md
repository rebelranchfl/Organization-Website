## Source audit conclusion
All ten sources were evaluated for the claim actually assigned to them. The core system conclusions are corroborated across USDA and multiple independent Cooperative Extension sources. No source is being used merely because of institutional prestige.

## Material evidence boundaries
- Commercial broiler systems provide unusually detailed evidence about closed-water function and failure modes, but their exact flow, pressure, stocking and line-management specifications are not universal backyard-flock specifications.
- Chemical sanitation/treatment methods discussed in commercial poultry sources are not copied into a generic RRA recipe. Source chemistry, system materials, label directions and flock context matter.
- "Automatic" does not mean maintenance-free or safe to leave unverified. This is an inference from the documented failure modes and maintenance requirements, not a quoted industry slogan.
- Freeze prevention is a design problem requiring climate/hardware research. Research supports the need for ice-free access and safe thawing boundaries; it does not support a universal improvised heater design.
- Owner source status may remain `Pending` because the Research Agent has completed the audit. Pending owner review is not a blocker unless the owner overrides a source or a material evidence conflict emerges.
