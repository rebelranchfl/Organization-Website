## Market / differentiation QA

The observed market includes:
- substantial free news/media-literacy resources;
- low-cost worksheets and short units;
- low-cost critical-thinking workbooks;
- premium multi-year digital-literacy curricula.

Therefore a generic fact/opinion curriculum would have weak differentiation. The recommended product survives QA because it is a cross-domain, repeat-use decision system rather than another reading/worksheet package.

## Failure conditions that Visual Production must not ignore

The design passes **only if** later implementation actually provides:

1. multiple simultaneous claim layers and pressure pathways;
2. working source-root/echo visualization;
3. persistent learner state through depth routes;
4. a living personalized My Decision Record;
5. real explanatory visuals;
6. scenario practice that requires reasoning rather than answer memorization/click-through;
7. no dead links/404s;
8. no claim that the software automatically determines truth or diagnoses bias;
9. no non-clickable pill-shaped labels/statuses;
10. an owner-review preview showing the exact integrated customer experience.

If these are absent, Final Product QA must FAIL regardless of closeout documentation.
