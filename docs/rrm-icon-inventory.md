# Rebel Ranch Ministries Icon Inventory

**Status:** Approved RRM public-site icon list
**Last updated:** 2026-09-24
**Governing visual rules:** [`rrm-visual-rules.md`](rrm-visual-rules.md)

## Standard construction

All icons listed here use the same RRM system:

- dark-green circular disk: `#173A26`;
- thin `2px` circle border: `#4A7C59`;
- gold linework: `#EF9F27`;
- green linework: `#97C459`;
- no white, burgundy, tan, brown, or bare-glyph variants inside the same page family; and
- consistent circle size and visual weight within each section.

The icon circle does not imply interaction. The surrounding card, row, or button must communicate whether something is clickable.

The expand/collapse control must use the complete SVG file. Do not substitute a text character or rebuild the circle and chevron separately in CSS. Rotate the complete SVG 180 degrees for the expanded state.

## Meaning boundaries

- **Rebel Ranch Academy** represents the program where learning happens.
- **Educators** represents the people who teach and guide.
- **Knowledge** represents what education and guidance strengthen.
- These three concepts must remain visually distinct.
- The outcome icon belongs inside the expanded source row that explains the connection. Do not require visitors to match a source list to a separate outcome diagram.

## Review checklist

Before approving a page:

1. Confirm every icon uses the same disk, border, colors, and line weight.
2. Confirm no icon disappears into its page or card background.
3. Confirm the same symbol is not being used for two different meanings.
4. Confirm all icons in one section are displayed at the same circle size.
5. Confirm expansion content places its related outcome icon beside the outcome explanation.
