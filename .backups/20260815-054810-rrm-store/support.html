The Cost Behind the Help

Real help isn’t free to give.

Business Freedom hours, Creation Station tools and hosting, Marketplace infrastructure, guided projects, automated invoicing — every one of these has a real bill behind it, even when we price it far below what it actually costs to build and run. We’re not a big-budget nonprofit. We’re a small ministry trying to give away more than we can technically afford, on purpose, because the families who need it most usually can’t pay full price either.

Your support doesn’t pad a budget somewhere. It’s what lets us recoup what this actually costs — and keep building the next program instead of quietly scaling back the one we already promised.

Every dollar keeps a real program running.
