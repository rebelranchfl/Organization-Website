Roots, Boots & Animal Poops

Growing food. Raising animals. Understanding the system.

Roots, Boots & Animal Poops is Rebel Ranch Ministries’ working agricultural program. Follow real livestock, produce, soil, water, food production, and natural systems in action.

LivestockAnimal care, husbandry, behavior, nutrition, breeding, lifecycles, and raising animals as part of a food system.
ProduceGrowing food, seasonal production, harvesting, using what we grow, and learning to work with the land available.
Soil & WaterFertility, manure, composting, regeneration, conservation, filtration, storage, and resilience.
Food SystemsWhere food comes from, how the pieces connect, preservation, nutrition, utilization, and reducing waste.

As the program grows, food and agricultural production can help support ministry work, feed people in the community, and open future opportunities for food shares and appropriate agricultural sales.

The real work becomes the lesson. Follow what we’re doing through videos, demonstrations, Academy learning, family activities, and digital resources you can use wherever you live.

Rebel Ranch Rescue — RRR

Rescue. Recover. Respect the animal.

Rebel Ranch Rescue focuses on farm animals that need rescue, recovery, responsible care, or appropriate long-term placement.

Rescue & RecoveryMeet immediate needs, support recovery, and give each animal the care its situation requires.
Farm-Animal FocusRRR is focused on livestock and farm animals rather than a general dog-and-cat rescue.
Responsible StewardshipLearn about care, housing, nutrition, behavior, ownership responsibility, and appropriate placement.
Learn From the StoryReal rescue experiences can teach care, recovery, behavior, lifecycles, and responsibility.

Two different kinds of animal work. Rescue animals are part of Rebel Ranch Rescue. Livestock intentionally raised through our agricultural food system are part of Roots, Boots & Animal Poops.
