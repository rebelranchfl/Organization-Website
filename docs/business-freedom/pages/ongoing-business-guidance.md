## Required professional-capability sections

### Operations Strategist

Evaluates ideas, opportunities, investments, priorities, services, and growth plans. Shows how a proposed direction fits the whole business before the owner commits resources.

### Operations Manager

Organizes work, responsibilities, vendors, workloads, timelines, and accountability. Helps turn the chosen direction into an operating plan the business can follow.

### Process Engineer

Analyzes operations, finds root causes, redesigns workflows, connects systems, and introduces appropriate automation. Shows how the pieces must work together rather than treating each problem in isolation.

These capabilities are distinct and must be explained clearly. They belong on one comprehensive page because the recurring service provides access to the combined professional capability, not three small commodity offers.
