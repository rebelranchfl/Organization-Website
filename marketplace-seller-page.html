About

Cypress Creek Apiary has been keeping bees along the Suwannee since 2019. Every jar is raw, unfiltered, and never heated past hive temperature — bottled within a week of harvest. We also make small-batch beeswax candles from our own cappings.

Currently offering: raw wildflower honey (8oz / 16oz / 32oz), comb honey by the piece, and hand-poured beeswax candles. Local pickup near Trenton, or delivery within Gilchrist and Levy counties.
